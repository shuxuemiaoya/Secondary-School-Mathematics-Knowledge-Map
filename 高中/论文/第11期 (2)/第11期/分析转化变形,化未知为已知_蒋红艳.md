# 1 利用诱导公式求值

例1 已知 $\tan \left(\alpha + \frac{8\pi}{7}\right) = m (m \neq -1)$ , 求代数式 $\frac{\sin \left(\frac{15\pi}{7} + \alpha\right) + 3\cos \left(\alpha - \frac{13\pi}{7}\right)}{\sin \left(\frac{20\pi}{7} - \alpha\right) - \cos \left(\alpha + \frac{22\pi}{7}\right)}$ 的值.

解: 原式

$$
\begin{array}{l} = \frac {\sin \left[ \pi + \left(\frac {8 \pi}{7} + \alpha\right) \right] + 3 \cos \left[ - 3 \pi + \left(\alpha + \frac {8 \pi}{7}\right) \right]}{\sin \left[ 4 \pi - \left(\frac {8 \pi}{7} + \alpha\right) \right] - \cos \left[ 2 \pi + \left(\alpha + \frac {8 \pi}{7}\right) \right]} \\ = \frac {- \sin \left(\frac {8 \pi}{7} + \alpha\right) + 3 \cos \left[ \pi - \left(\alpha + \frac {8 \pi}{7}\right) \right]}{- \sin \left(\frac {8 \pi}{7} + \alpha\right) - \cos \left(\alpha + \frac {8 \pi}{7}\right)} \\ = \frac {\sin \left(\frac {8 \pi}{7} + \alpha\right) + 3 \cos \left(\alpha + \frac {8 \pi}{7}\right)}{\sin \left(\frac {8 \pi}{7} + \alpha\right) + \cos \left(\alpha + \frac {8 \pi}{7}\right)} \\ = \frac {\tan \left(\frac {8 \pi}{7} + \alpha\right) + 3}{\tan \left(\frac {8 \pi}{7} + \alpha\right) + 1} \\ = \frac {m + 3}{m + 1}. \\ \end{array}
$$

思路与方法:本题灵活运用了整体思想,把 $\alpha+\frac{8\pi}{7}$ 当成一个角,用诱导公式把其他角的三角函数全都化为 $\alpha+\frac{8\pi}{7}$ 的三角函数值;当然,本题也可以先将 $\tan\left(\alpha+\frac{8\pi}{7}\right)=m$ 化为 $\tan\left(\alpha+\frac{\pi}{7}\right)=m$ ,然后用诱导公式把其他角的三角函数都化为 $\alpha +\frac{\pi}{7}$ 的三角函数.

# 2 利用诱导公式化简

例2 化简： $\sin \left(k\pi -\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)\cos \left(k\pi +\frac{\pi}{6}\right)(k\in \mathbf{Z}).$

解:(1)当 $k=2n(n\in\mathbf{Z})$ 时，

原式 $= \sin \left(2n\pi -\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)\cos \left(2n\pi +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = -\sin \frac{2\pi}{3}\cdot$

$$
\cos {\frac {\pi}{6}} = - \sin {\frac {\pi}{3}} \cos {\frac {\pi}{6}} = - \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} \times \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2} = - \frac {3}{4}.
$$

(2) 当 $k = 2n + 1 (n \in \mathbf{Z})$ 时，

原式 $= \sin \left(2n\pi +\pi -\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)\cos \left(2n\pi +\pi +\frac{\pi}{6}\right) =$

$$
\sin \frac {\pi}{3} \cos \left(\pi + \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = \sin \frac {\pi}{3} \left(- \cos \frac {\pi}{6}\right) = - \frac {3}{4}.
$$

所以 $\sin\left(k\pi-\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)\cos\left(k\pi+\frac{\pi}{6}\right)=-\frac{3}{4}(k\in\mathbf{Z})$ .

思路与方法:本题在灵活运用诱导公式化简的过程中,主要体现了转化思想.

# 3 利用诱导公式证明

例3 证明: $\sec \alpha - \tan \alpha = \tan \left( \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{\alpha}{2} \right)$ .

法 1: $\sec \alpha - \tan \alpha = \frac{1}{\cos \alpha} - \frac{\sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} = \frac{1 - \sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} =$

$$
\frac {\sin^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2} + \cos^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2} - 2 \sin \frac {\alpha}{2} \cos \frac {\alpha}{2}}{\cos^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2} - \sin^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2}} = \frac {\left(\cos \frac {\alpha}{2} - \sin \frac {\alpha}{2}\right) ^ {2}}{\cos^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2} - \sin^ {2} \frac {\alpha}{2}} =
$$

$$
\frac {\cos \frac {\alpha}{2} - \sin \frac {\alpha}{2}}{\cos \frac {\alpha}{2} + \sin \frac {\alpha}{2}} = \frac {1 - \tan \frac {\alpha}{2}}{1 + \tan \frac {\alpha}{2}} = \tan \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \frac {\alpha}{2}\right).
$$

法 2: $\sec\alpha - \tan\alpha = \frac{1 - \sin\alpha}{\cos\alpha} = \frac{1 - \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2} - \alpha\right)}{\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2} - \alpha\right)} =$

$$
\tan \left[ \frac {1}{2} \left(\frac {\pi}{2} - \alpha\right) \right] = \tan \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \frac {\alpha}{2}\right).
$$

法 3:sec $\alpha-\tan\alpha$

$$
\begin{array}{l} = \frac {1 - \sin \alpha}{\cos \alpha} \\ = \frac {2 \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \frac {\alpha}{2}\right) \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \frac {\alpha}{2}\right)}{\cos \alpha} \\ = \frac {2 \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{4} + \frac {\alpha}{2}\right) \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \frac {\alpha}{2}\right)}{2 \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \frac {\alpha}{2}\right) \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \frac {\alpha}{2}\right)} \\ = \tan \left(\frac {\pi}{4} - \frac {\alpha}{2}\right). \\ \end{array}
$$

思路与方法:本题的三种证法主要体现了运用诱导公式与三角公式进行恒等变换的技巧;解题的关键是要熟悉诱导公式,会根据解题的需要作适当的变形,还要具有敏锐的观察力与逻辑推导能力.

# 4 诱导公式的逆用

例 4 在 $\triangle ABC$ 中, 若 $\sin C$ 是 $\sin A$ 与 $\sin B$ 的等差中项, 证明 $\sin(A-B)=2(\sin A-\sin B)$ .

证明:由 $\sin A + \sin B = 2\sin C$ ，得 $2\sin \frac{A + B}{2} \cdot \cos \frac{A - B}{2} = 4\sin \frac{A + B}{2} \cos \frac{A + B}{2}$ ，则 $\cos \frac{A - B}{2} = 2\cos \frac{A + B}{2}$ ，等式两边同乘 $2\sin \frac{A - B}{2}$ ，得 $2\sin \frac{A - B}{2} \cdot \cos \frac{A - B}{2} = 4\sin \frac{A - B}{2} \cos \frac{A + B}{2}$ ，即

$$
2 \sin \frac {A - B}{2} \cos \frac {A - B}{2} = 2 \left(2 \sin \frac {A - B}{2} \cdot \cos \frac {A + B}{2}\right).
$$

所以 $\sin(A-B)=2(\sin A-\sin B)$ .

思路与方法:本题如果直接证明比较困难,不如先进行分析, $\sin(A-B)=2(\sin A-\sin B)\Rightarrow2\sin\frac{A-B}{2}\cos\frac{A-B}{2}=4\cos\frac{A+B}{2}\sin\frac{A-B}{2}\Rightarrow\cos\frac{A-B}{2}=2\cos\frac{A+B}{2}$ ;再由条件步步逆推, $\sin A+\sin B=2\sin C\Rightarrow2\sin\frac{A+B}{2}\cos\frac{A-B}{2}=2\sin(A+B)=4\sin\frac{A+B}{2}\cos\frac{A+B}{2}\Rightarrow\cos\frac{A-B}{2}=2\cos\frac{A+B}{2}$ .这样,顺推与逆推相结合,达到了殊途同归的目的.
