##### 【典例1】（23-24高一下·江苏南京·期中）$\cos 2 4 {^\circ} \cos 6 9 {^\circ} + \sin 2 4 {^\circ} \sin 1 11 {^\circ} =$（ ）
$\quad$A．$- \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ 
$\quad$B． 
$\quad$C．$- \frac{1}{2}$ 
$\quad$D．$\frac{1}{2}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>B
<span class="fake-tag">分析</span>由诱导公式和两角差的余弦公式求解即可.
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>$\cos 2 4 {^\circ} \cos 6 9 {^\circ} + \sin 2 4 {^\circ} \sin 1 11 {^\circ} = \cos 2 4 {^\circ} \cos 6 9 {^\circ} + \sin 2 4 {^\circ} \sin ( 180 {^\circ} - 69 {^\circ} )$
$= \cos 2 4 {^\circ} \cos 6 9 {^\circ} + \sin 2 4 {^\circ} \sin 6 9 {^\circ} = \cos ( 69 {^\circ} - 24 {^\circ} ) = \cos 4 5 {^\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$.
故选：B.

##### 【典例3】（23-24高一上·宁夏银川·期末）求下列各式的值.
已知$\alpha \in \left( - \frac{\pi}{4} , 0 \right) , \beta \in \left( \frac{\pi}{2} , \pi \right)$，$\text{cos} ( \alpha + \beta ) = - \frac{4}{5} , \text{cos} \left( \beta - \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = \frac{5}{13}$，求$\text{cos} \left( \alpha + \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$的值.
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$\frac{16}{65}$
<span class="fake-tag">分析</span>利用整体法，结合三角函数的基本关系式与和差公式即可得解.
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$\alpha \in \left( - \frac{\pi}{4} , 0 \right) , \beta \in \left( \frac{\pi}{2} , \pi \right)$，
则$\alpha + \beta \in \left( \frac{\pi}{4} , \pi \right)$，$\beta - \frac{\pi}{4} \in \left( \frac{\pi}{4} , \frac{3 \pi}{4} \right)$，
又$\text{cos} ( \alpha + \beta ) = - \frac{4}{5} , \text{cos} \left( \beta - \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = \frac{5}{13}$，
所以$\sin ( \alpha + \beta ) = \sqrt{1 - \text{co} \text{s}^{2} ( \alpha + \beta )} = \frac{3}{5}$，$\sin \left( \beta - \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = \sqrt{1 - \text{co} \text{s}^{2} \left( \beta - \frac{\pi}{4} \right)} = \frac{12}{13}$，
则$\cos \left( \alpha + \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = \cos \left\lbrack \alpha + \beta - \left( \beta - \frac{\pi}{4} \right) \right\rbrack$
$= \cos ( \alpha + \beta ) \cos \left( \beta - \frac{\pi}{4} \right) + \sin ( \alpha + \beta ) \sin \left( \beta - \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$= $- \frac{4}{5} \times \frac{5}{13} + \frac{3}{5} \times \frac{12}{13} = \frac{16}{65}$.$
##### 【变式1】（23-24高二上·四川南充·期中）若$\alpha \in [ 0 , \pi ]$，$\cos \frac{5 \alpha}{3} \cos \frac{2 \alpha}{3} + \sin \frac{5 \alpha}{3} \sin \frac{2 \alpha}{3} = 0$，则$\alpha$的值是（ ）
$\quad$A．$\frac{\pi}{6}$ 
$\quad$B．$\frac{\pi}{4}$ 
$\quad$C．$\frac{\pi}{3}$ 
$\quad$D．$\frac{\pi}{2}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>D
<span class="fake-tag">分析</span>利用余弦的差角公式得到$\text{cos} \alpha = 0$，再根据条件即可求出结果.
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$\cos \frac{5 \alpha}{3} \cos \frac{2 \alpha}{3} + \sin \frac{5 \alpha}{3} \sin \frac{2 \alpha}{3} = \text{cos} ( \frac{5 \alpha}{3} - \frac{2 \alpha}{3} ) = \text{cos} \alpha = 0$，
又$\alpha \in [ 0 , \pi ]$，所以$\alpha = \frac{\pi}{2}$，
故选：D.
##### 【变式2】（23-24高一上·全国·课后作业）已知$\cos \alpha = \frac{4}{5}$，$\cos ( \alpha - \beta ) = - \frac{4}{5}$，$\frac{3 \pi}{2} < \alpha < 2 \pi$，$\frac{\pi}{2} < \alpha - \beta < \pi$，则 .
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$- 1$

<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>由已知得：$\sin \alpha = - \frac{3}{5}$，$\sin ( \alpha - \beta ) = \frac{3}{5}$，

故答案为：$- 1$.
