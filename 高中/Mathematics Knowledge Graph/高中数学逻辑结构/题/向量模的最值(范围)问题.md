##### 题型08向量模的最值（范围）问题
##### 【典例1】（2023上·云南曲靖·高三曲靖一中校考阶段练习）已知向量$\overrightarrow{a} = ( 1 , 0 )$，$\overrightarrow{b} = \left( 1 , - \sqrt{3} \right)$，若非零向量$\overrightarrow{c}$满足$\left\langle \overrightarrow{c} , \overrightarrow{a} \right\rangle = \left\langle \overrightarrow{c} , \overrightarrow{b} \right\rangle$，则$\left| \overrightarrow{c} - \overrightarrow{a} \right|$取最小值时，$\overrightarrow{c}$的坐标为 .
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$\left( \frac{3}{4} , - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \right)$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>设$\overrightarrow{c} = ( x , y )$，
则由$\left\langle \overrightarrow{c} , \overrightarrow{a} \right\rangle = \left\langle \overrightarrow{c} , \overrightarrow{b} \right\rangle$，得$\frac{\overrightarrow{c} \cdot \overrightarrow{a}}{\left| \overrightarrow{c} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|} = \frac{\overrightarrow{c} \cdot \overrightarrow{b}}{\left| \overrightarrow{c} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|}$，所以$\frac{\overrightarrow{c} \cdot \overrightarrow{a}}{\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|} = \frac{\overrightarrow{c} \cdot \overrightarrow{b}}{\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|}$，
所以$\frac{( x , y ) \cdot ( 1 , 0 )}{1} = \frac{( x , y ) \cdot \left( 1 , - \sqrt{3} \right)}{2}$，即$x = \frac{x - \sqrt{3} y}{2}$，化得$x = - \sqrt{3} y$.
又$\overrightarrow{c} - \overrightarrow{a} = ( x - 1 , y )$，
所以$\left| \overrightarrow{c} - \overrightarrow{a} \right| = \sqrt{( x - 1 )^{2} + y^{2}} = \sqrt{\frac{4}{3} \left( x - \frac{3}{4} \right)^{2} + \frac{1}{4}}$.
当$x = \frac{3}{4}$时，$\left| \overrightarrow{c} - \overrightarrow{a} \right|$取得最小值$\frac{1}{2}$，
此时$y = - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$，即$\overrightarrow{c} = \left( \frac{3}{4} , - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \right)$.
故答案为：$\left( \frac{3}{4} , - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \right)$.
