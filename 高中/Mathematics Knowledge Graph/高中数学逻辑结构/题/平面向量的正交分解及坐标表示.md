##### 【典例1】（2023·广西·校联考模拟预测）已知$\overrightarrow{i}$和$\overrightarrow{j}$是两个正交单位向量，$\overrightarrow{a} = 2 \overrightarrow{i} + 3 \overrightarrow{j}$，$\overrightarrow{b} = \overrightarrow{i} + k \overrightarrow{j}$且$\left| \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right| = \sqrt{2}$，则$k =$（ ）
$\quad$A．2或3 
$\quad$B．2或4 
$\quad$C．3或5 
$\quad$D．3或4
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>B
<span class="fake-tag">分析</span>根据题意得到$\overrightarrow{a} = ( 2 , 3 )$，$\overrightarrow{b} = ( 1 , k )$，求得$\overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} = ( 1 , 3 - k )$，集合向量模的计算公式，列出方程，即可求解.
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$\overrightarrow{i}$和$\overrightarrow{j}$是正交单位向量，$\overrightarrow{a} = 2 \overrightarrow{i} + 3 \overrightarrow{j} = ( 2 , 3 )$，$\overrightarrow{b} = \overrightarrow{i} + k \overrightarrow{j} = ( 1 , k )$，
可得$\overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} = ( 1 , 3 - k )$，所以$\left| \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right| = \sqrt{1 + ( 3 - k )^{2}} = \sqrt{2}$，解得$k = 2$或$k = 4$.
故选：B．

##### 【变式3】（2022·山东·高三专题练习）在平面直角坐标系$x O y$中，点$P \left( \sqrt{3} , 1 \right)$，将向量$\overrightarrow{O P}$绕点$O$按逆时针方向旋转$\frac{\pi}{2}$后得到向量，则点$Q$的坐标是（ ）
$\quad$A．$\left( - \sqrt{2} , 1 \right)$ 
$\quad$B．$\left( - 1 , \sqrt{2} \right)$ 
$\quad$C．$\left( - \sqrt{3} , 1 \right)$

<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>D

<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>由$P \left( \sqrt{3} , 1 \right)$，得$P \left( 2 \cos \frac{\pi}{6} , 2 \sin \frac{\pi}{6} \right)$，
