##### 题型09向量夹角最值（范围问题）
##### 【典例1】（2022上·上海宝山·高二上海交大附中校考阶段练习）若平面向量$\overrightarrow{a}$，$\overrightarrow{b}$，$\overrightarrow{c}$满足$\left| \overrightarrow{c} \right| = 1$，$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{c} = 1$，$\overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{c} = 3$，$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 2$，则$\overrightarrow{a}$，$\overrightarrow{b}$夹角的取值范围是（ ）
$\quad$A．$\left. \frac{\pi}{6} , \frac{\pi}{2} \right)$ 
$\quad$B．$\left. \frac{\pi}{6} , \pi \right)$ 
$\quad$C．$\left. \frac{\pi}{3} , \frac{\pi}{2} \right)$ 
$\quad$D．$\left. \frac{\pi}{3} , \pi \right)$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>C
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>设$\overrightarrow{O A} = \overrightarrow{a}$，$\overrightarrow{O B} = \overrightarrow{b}$，$\overrightarrow{O C} = \overrightarrow{c}$，以*O*为原点，$\overrightarrow{c}$方向为*x*轴正方向建立平面直角坐标系，
$\because \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{c} = 1 > 0$，$\overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{c} = 3 > 0$，$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 2 > 0$，
$\therefore \overrightarrow{a}$，$\overrightarrow{b}$，$\overrightarrow{c}$三者直接各自的夹角都为锐角，
$\because \left| \overrightarrow{c} \right| = 1$，$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{c} = \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow{c} \right| \cos \left\langle \overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{c} \right\rangle = 1$，$\overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{c} = \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow{c} \right| \cos \left\langle \overrightarrow{b} , \overrightarrow{c} \right\rangle = 3$，
$\therefore \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| \cos \left\langle \overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{c} \right\rangle = 1$，$\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| \cos \left\langle \overrightarrow{b} , \overrightarrow{c} \right\rangle = 3$，即$\overrightarrow{a}$在$\overrightarrow{c}$上的投影为1，$\overrightarrow{b}$在$\overrightarrow{c}$上的投影为3，
$\therefore A ( 1 , m )$，$B ( 3 , n )$，如图

$\therefore \overrightarrow{a} = ( 1 , m )$，$\overrightarrow{b} = ( 3 , n )$\therefore \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 3 + m n = 2$即$m n = - 1$，且$\cos \left\langle \overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b} \right\rangle = \frac{\left| \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} \right|}{\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{1 + m^{2}} \cdot \sqrt{9 + n^{2}}}$

##### 【典例2】（2020上·上海徐汇·高二位育中学校考期中）已知$A D$为△$A B C$边$B C$上的中线，点$G$满足$\overrightarrow{A G} = 3 \overrightarrow{G D}$且$\overrightarrow{G A} \cdot \overrightarrow{G B} = 0$，则$\cos C$的最小值为 .

<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>由$\overrightarrow{A G} = 3 \overrightarrow{G D}$且$\overrightarrow{G A} \cdot \overrightarrow{G B} = 0$，则$A G = 3 G D , G A \bot B G$，构建如下平面直角坐标系，

*G*为原点，结合中线可令，$B ( m , 0 )$，$C ( - m , - 2 )$，则$\overrightarrow{C A} = ( m , 5 )$，$\overrightarrow{C B} = ( 2 m , 2 )$，
∴$\cos C = \frac{\overrightarrow{C A} \cdot \overrightarrow{C B}}{| \overrightarrow{C A} | \cdot | \overrightarrow{C B} |} = \frac{m^{2} + 5}{\sqrt{m^{2} + 2 5} \cdot \sqrt{m^{2} + 1}} = \sqrt{\frac{m^{4} + 1 0 m^{2} + 2 5}{m^{4} + 2 6 m^{2} + 2 5}} = \sqrt{1 - \frac{16}{m^{2} + \frac{25}{m^{2}} + 2 6}}$，

##### 【变式1】（2021下·江苏·高一期中）设$\overrightarrow{e_{1}} , \overrightarrow{e_{2}}$为单位向量，满足$\left| 2 \overrightarrow{e_{1}} - \overrightarrow{e_{2}} \right| \leq \sqrt{2} , \overrightarrow{a} = \overrightarrow{e_{1}} + \overrightarrow{e_{2}} , \overrightarrow{b} = 3 \overrightarrow{e_{1}} + \overrightarrow{e_{2}}$，设$\overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b}$的夹角为$\theta$，则${} {\cos {}^{2}} \theta$的可能取值为（ ）

当$x = \frac{3}{4}$时，$\frac{28}{29}$，
