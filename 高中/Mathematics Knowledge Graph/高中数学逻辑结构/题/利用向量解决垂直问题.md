![@@@4d9cf4f6-ae4e-4b8c-b8a6-4904e859af32](MathLogic-Weave/高中数学逻辑结构/题/Attachments/856fa12cfb3bebe855e217c410a90ff0.png)

##### 【变式2】（2023下·陕西西安·高一统考期末）已知在$\bigtriangleup A B C$中，点$M$是$B C$边上靠近点$B$的四等分点，点$N$为$A B$中点，设$A M$与$C N$相交于点$P$.
![@@@bb142b18-d42f-4bb2-ba74-1f903a649d71](MathLogic-Weave/高中数学逻辑结构/题/Attachments/121dc4d2bda3c38045c99d873204fa98.jpeg)
(1)请用$\overrightarrow{A B}$、$\overrightarrow{A C}$表示向量$\overrightarrow{A M}$；
(2)设$\overrightarrow{A B}$和$\overrightarrow{A C}$的夹角为$\theta$，若$\cos \theta = \frac{1}{4}$，且$\left| \overrightarrow{A C} \right| = 2 \left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right|$，求证：$\overrightarrow{C N} \bot \overrightarrow{A B}$.
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>(1)$\overrightarrow{A M} = \frac{3}{4} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{4} \overrightarrow{A C}$.
(2)证明见解析.
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>（1）$\overrightarrow{A M} = \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{B M} = \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{4} \overrightarrow{B C} = \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{4} ( \overrightarrow{A C} - \overrightarrow{A B} ) = \frac{3}{4} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{4} \overrightarrow{A C}$.
（2）$\overrightarrow{C N} = \overrightarrow{A N} - \overrightarrow{A C} = \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A B} - \overrightarrow{A C}$，
$\overrightarrow{C N} \cdot \overrightarrow{A B} = \left( \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A B} - \overrightarrow{A C} \right) \cdot \overrightarrow{A B} = \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A B}^{2} - \overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A C} = \frac{1}{2} \left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right|^{2} - \left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow{A C} \right| \cos \theta = \frac{1}{2} \left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right|^{2} - \left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right| \cdot 2 \left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right| \cdot \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1}{2} \left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right|^{2} - \left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right| \cdot 2 \left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right| \cdot \frac{1}{4} = 0$，$\therefore \overrightarrow{C N} \bot \overrightarrow{A B}$.
