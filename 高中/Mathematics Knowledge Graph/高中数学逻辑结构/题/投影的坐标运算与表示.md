##### 题型04 向量的投影
##### 【典例1】（2023·全国·模拟预测）向量$\overrightarrow{a} = ( 1 , 2 )$，$\overrightarrow{b} = ( - 2 , - 1 )$，那么向量$\overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b}$在$\overrightarrow{a}$上的投影向量为（ ）
$\quad$A．$\left( \frac{9}{5} , \frac{18}{5} \right)$ 
$\quad$B．$\left( \frac{1}{5} , \frac{2}{5} \right)$
$\quad$C． 
$\quad$D．
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>A
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$\overrightarrow{a} = ( 1 , 2 )$，$\overrightarrow{b} = ( - 2 , - 1 )$，所以$\overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} = ( 3 , 3 )$，
则$\overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b}$在$\overrightarrow{a}$上的投影向量的模为
$\cos \left\langle \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} , \overrightarrow{a} \right\rangle \cdot \left| \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right| = \frac{\left( \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right) \cdot \overrightarrow{a}}{\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|} = \frac{9}{\sqrt{5}} = \frac{9 \sqrt{5}}{5}$，
则$\overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b}$在$\overrightarrow{a}$上的投影向量为$\frac{9 \sqrt{5}}{5} \cdot \frac{\overrightarrow{a}}{\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|} = \left( \frac{9}{5} , \frac{18}{5} \right)$.
故选：A．
##### 【典例2】（2023上·上海静安·高三校考阶段练习）已知向量，$\overrightarrow{b} = ( - 3 , 1 )$且$\left( 2 \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} \right) \bot \overrightarrow{b}$，则向量$\overrightarrow{a}$在向量$\overrightarrow{b}$方向上的投影向量为 .
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$\left( \frac{3}{2} , - \frac{1}{2} \right)$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为，$\overrightarrow{b} = ( - 3 , 1 )$，
则$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = - 3 + t$，$\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| = \sqrt{( - 3 )^{2} + 1^{2}} = \sqrt{10}$，
又$\left( 2 \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} \right) \bot \overrightarrow{b}$，所以$\left( 2 \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} \right) \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 2 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} + \overrightarrow{b}^{2} = 0$，
即$2 ( - 3 + t ) + 1 0 = 0$，解得$t = - 2$，
所以$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = - 5$，
则向量$\overrightarrow{a}$在向量$\overrightarrow{b}$方向上的投影向量为$\frac{\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b}}{\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|} \cdot \frac{\overrightarrow{b}}{\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|} = \frac{- 5}{\sqrt{10}} \cdot \frac{( - 3 , 1 )}{\sqrt{10}} = \left( \frac{3}{2} , - \frac{1}{2} \right)$.
故答案为：$\left( \frac{3}{2} , - \frac{1}{2} \right)$
##### 【变式1】（2023·上海杨浦·统考一模）已知向量$\overrightarrow{a} = ( 3 , 0 )$，$\overrightarrow{b} = \left( - 2 , 2 \sqrt{3} \right)$，则$\overrightarrow{b}$在$\overrightarrow{a}$方向上的投影为 .
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$- 2$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>向量$\overrightarrow{a} = ( 3 , 0 )$，$\overrightarrow{b} = \left( - 2 , 2 \sqrt{3} \right)$，
则$\overrightarrow{b}$在$\overrightarrow{a}$方向上的投影为$\frac{\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b}}{\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|} = \frac{- 6}{3} = - 2$.
故答案为：$- 2$
##### 【变式2】（2024上·云南·高三云南省下关第一中学校联考阶段练习）已知向量$\overrightarrow{a} = ( 5 , 2 )$，$\overrightarrow{b} = ( - 1 , 3 )$，则向量$\overrightarrow{a}$在向量$\overrightarrow{b}$上的投影向量的坐标为 .
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$\left( - \frac{1}{10} , \frac{3}{10} \right)$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为向量$\overrightarrow{a} = ( 5 , 2 )$，$\overrightarrow{b} = ( - 1 , 3 )$，
所以$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 5 \times ( - 1 ) + 2 \times 3 = 1$，$\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| = \sqrt{( - 1 )^{2} + 3^{2}} = \sqrt{10}$，
所以向量$\overrightarrow{a}$在向量$\overrightarrow{b}$上的投影向量的坐标为：$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| \cdot \frac{\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b}}{\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|} \cdot \frac{\overrightarrow{b}}{\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|} = \frac{1}{10} \cdot ( - 1 , 3 ) = \left( - \frac{1}{10} , \frac{3}{10} \right)$.
故答案为：$\left( - \frac{1}{10} , \frac{3}{10} \right)$.
