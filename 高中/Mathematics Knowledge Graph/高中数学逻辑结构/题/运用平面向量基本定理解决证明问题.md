##### 【典例2】（2023下·河北保定·高一校联考期中）已知$m > 0 , n > 0$，如图，在$\bigtriangleup A B C$中，点$M , N$满足$\overrightarrow{A M} = m \overrightarrow{A B}$，$\overrightarrow{A N} = n \overrightarrow{A C} , D$是线段$B C$上一点，$\overrightarrow{B D} = \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{B C}$，点$E$为$A D$的中点，且$M , N , E$三点共线．

<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>（1）由题可知$\overrightarrow{A D} = \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{B D} = \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{B C} = \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{3} \left( \overrightarrow{A C} - \overrightarrow{A B} \right) = \frac{2}{3} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{A C}$，
因为点$E$为$A D$的中点，所以$\overrightarrow{A E} = \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A D} = \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{6} \overrightarrow{A C} .$

所以$3 m + 6 n$的最小值为4.

##### 【变式1】（2023下·福建漳州·高一统考期末）如图，在$\bigtriangleup A B C$中，$A D = \frac{1}{3} A B$，点$E$是$C D$的中点，设$\overrightarrow{A B} = \overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{A C} = \overrightarrow{b}$，
![@@@e700f1f8-2aa7-44f7-be33-ef59e6e6ae45](高中习题/课堂同步/随堂练习习题/必修二/Attachments/ad74990b61c4acbd08ab4cfc44cc2b08.png)
(1)用$\overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b}$表示$\overrightarrow{C D} , \overrightarrow{A E}$；
(2)如果$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| = 3 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|$，$C D , A E$有什么位置关系？用向量方法证明你的结论.
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>(1)$\overrightarrow{C D} = \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b}$，$\overrightarrow{A E} = \frac{1}{6} \overrightarrow{a} + \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{b} .$
(2)$C D \bot A E$，证明见解析
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>（1）解：因为$A D = \frac{1}{3} A B$，所以$\overrightarrow{C D} = \overrightarrow{A D} - \overrightarrow{A C} = \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{A B} - \overrightarrow{A C} = \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b}$，
因为$E$是$C D$的中点，
可得$\overrightarrow{A E} = \frac{1}{2} \left( \overrightarrow{A D} + \overrightarrow{A C} \right) = \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{A C} \right) = \frac{1}{6} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A C} = \frac{1}{6} \overrightarrow{a} + \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{b} .$
（2）解：$C D \bot A E$.
因为$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| = 3 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|$，
则$\overrightarrow{C D} \cdot \overrightarrow{A E} = \left( \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right) \cdot \left( \frac{1}{6} \overrightarrow{a} + \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{b} \right) = \frac{1}{18} \overrightarrow{a}^{2} + \frac{1}{6} \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} - \frac{1}{6} \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} - \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{b}^{2} = \frac{1}{18} \overrightarrow{a}^{2} - \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{b}^{2}$
$= \frac{1}{18} | \overrightarrow{a} |^{2} - \frac{1}{2} | \overrightarrow{b} |^{2} = \frac{1}{18} ( 3 | \overrightarrow{b} | )^{2} - \frac{1}{2} | \overrightarrow{b} |^{2} = 0$
以$\overrightarrow{C D} \bot \overrightarrow{A E}$，所以$C D \bot A E$.
##### 【变式2】（2023下·河南南阳·高一社旗县第一高级中学校联考期末）如图，在$\bigtriangleup A B C$中，$\overrightarrow{C D} = 2 \overrightarrow{D B} , \overrightarrow{A E} = \overrightarrow{E C}$．
![@@@d10fe4b3-bf53-4bd2-a894-146b8452296d](高中习题/课堂同步/随堂练习习题/必修二/Attachments/9b60466914b3cbb093f525a4463562a6.png)
(1)用$\overrightarrow{A B}$，$\overrightarrow{A D}$表示$\overrightarrow{A C}$，$\overrightarrow{B E}$；
(2)若点$M$满足$\overrightarrow{A M} = - \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{3}{4} \overrightarrow{A C}$，证明：$B$，$M$，$E$三点共线．
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>(1)$\overrightarrow{A C} = - 2 \overrightarrow{A B} + 3 \overrightarrow{A D}$，$\overrightarrow{B E} = - 2 \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{3}{2} \overrightarrow{A D}$
(2)证明见解析
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>（1）因为$\overrightarrow{C D} = 2 \overrightarrow{D B} , \overrightarrow{A E} = \overrightarrow{E C}$，
$\overrightarrow{A C} = \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{B C} = \overrightarrow{A B} + 3 \overrightarrow{B D}$
$= \overrightarrow{A B} + 3 \left( \overrightarrow{A D} - \overrightarrow{A B} \right) = - 2 \overrightarrow{A B} + 3 \overrightarrow{A D}$，
$\overrightarrow{B E} = \overrightarrow{B A} + \overrightarrow{A E} = - \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A C}$
$= - \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{2} \left( \overrightarrow{B C} - \overrightarrow{B A} \right) = - \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{B C}$
$= - \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{2} \times 3 \overrightarrow{B D} = - \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{2} \times 3 \left( \overrightarrow{A D} - \overrightarrow{A B} \right)$
$= - 2 \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{3}{2} \overrightarrow{A D}$.
（2）由$\overrightarrow{A M} = - \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{3}{4} \overrightarrow{A C}$，
可得$\overrightarrow{A M} = - \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{3}{4} \times 2 \overrightarrow{A E} = - \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{3}{2} \overrightarrow{A E}$，
所以$2 \overrightarrow{A M} = - \overrightarrow{A B} + 3 \overrightarrow{A E}$，$\overrightarrow{A E} - \overrightarrow{A B} = 2 \left( \overrightarrow{A M} - \overrightarrow{A E} \right)$，即$\overrightarrow{B E} = 2 \overrightarrow{E M}$，
所以$B$，$M$，$E$三点共线.
