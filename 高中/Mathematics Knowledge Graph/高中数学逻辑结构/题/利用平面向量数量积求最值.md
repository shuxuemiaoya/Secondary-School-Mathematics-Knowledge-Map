$\quad$A．2 
$\quad$B． 
$\quad$C．$\sqrt{2}$ 
$\quad$D．1
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>B
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$\left| \overrightarrow{a} + 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} = \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|^{2} + 4 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} + 4 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 4 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} - 4 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| + 4 = \left( 2 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| - 1 \right)^{2} + 3 \geq 3$，

##### 【典例4】（2023·上海崇明·统考一模）已知不平行的两个向量$\overrightarrow{a} , \mspace{6mu} \overrightarrow{b}$满足$\left|  \overrightarrow{a} \right| = 1$，$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = \sqrt{3}$．若对任意的$t \in R$，都有$\left| \overrightarrow{b} - t \overrightarrow{a} \right| \geq 2$成立，则$\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|$的最小值等于 ．
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$\sqrt{7}$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>依题意，设$\overrightarrow{a}$与$\overrightarrow{b}$的夹角为$\theta ( 0 \leq \theta \leq \pi )$，$\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| = m ( m > 0 )$，
因为$\left|  \overrightarrow{a} \right| = 1$，$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = \sqrt{3}$，所以$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| \cos \theta = \sqrt{3}$，即$m \cdot \cos \theta = \sqrt{3}$，
则$\cos \theta = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{m} = [ - 1 , 1 ]$，所以$m \geq \sqrt{3}$，
因为对任意的$t \in R$，都有$\left| \overrightarrow{b} - t \overrightarrow{a} \right| \geq 2$成立，
所以$\left( \overrightarrow{b} - t \overrightarrow{a} \right)^{2} \geq 4$，即$\overrightarrow{b}^{2} - 2 t \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} + t^{2} \overrightarrow{a}^{2} \geq 4$，即$t^{2} - 2 \sqrt{3} t + m^{2} - 4 \geq 0$对于$t \in R$恒成立，
故$\Delta = \left( 2 \sqrt{3} \right)^{2} - 4 \left( m^{2} - 4 \right) \leq 0$，又$m > 0$，解得$m \geq \sqrt{7}$，
综上，$m \geq \sqrt{7}$，则$\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|$的最小值为$\sqrt{7}$.
故答案为：$\sqrt{7}$.
##### 【变式1】（2023下·宁夏石嘴山·高一石嘴山市第三中学校考期末）已知向量$\overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b}$满足$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| = 1$，$\overrightarrow{b}$在$\overrightarrow{a}$方向上的投影向量为$\overrightarrow{a}$，则$\left| \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} \right|$的最小值为
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>2
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>由题意得$\frac{\overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{a}}{\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|} = \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|$，即$\overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{a} = \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|^{2} = 1$，
由于$\overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{a} > 0$，设$\overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b}$之间的夹角为，
即$\overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{a} = \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| \cos \theta = \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| \cos \theta = 1$，
故$\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| = \frac{1}{\cos \theta} \in \left. 1 , + \infty )$，
