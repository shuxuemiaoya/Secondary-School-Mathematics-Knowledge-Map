##### 题型08$\mathbf{1} \mathbf{5}^{\mathbf{\circ}}$（$\mathbf{7} \mathbf{5}^{\mathbf{\circ}}$）的正弦值
##### 【典例1】（23-24高三·湖南长沙·阶段练习）$\sin 1 95 {^\circ} =$（ ）
$\quad$A．$\frac{\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{6}}{4}$ 
$\quad$B．$\frac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4}$ 
$\quad$C．$\frac{\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{3}}{4}$ 
$\quad$D．$\frac{\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2}}{4}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>A
<span class="fake-tag">分析</span>利用两角和的正弦公式, 将$\sin 1 95 {^\circ}$化简,利用特殊角的三角函数值代入即可求得.
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>法一：$\sin 1 95 {^\circ} = \sin ( 180 {^\circ} + 1 5 {^\circ} ) = - \sin 1 5 {^\circ} = - \sin ( 45 {^\circ} - 30 {^\circ} )$
$= - \left( \sin 4 5 {^\circ} \cos 3 0 {^\circ} - \sin 3 0 {^\circ} \cos 4 5 {^\circ} \right) = - \left( \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right) = \frac{\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{6}}{4}$.
法二：$\sin 1 95 {^\circ} = \sin ( 135 {^\circ} + 6 0 {^\circ} ) = \sin 1 35 {^\circ} \cos 6 0 {^\circ} + \sin 6 0 {^\circ} \cos 1 35 {^\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \cdot \left( - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right) = \frac{\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{6}}{4}$
故选:$A$.
【点睛】本题考查两角和的正弦公式,关键在于熟记公式,准确化简,难度较易.

$\sin 7 5 {^\circ} =$ ； $\sin 1 5 {^\circ} =$ .

##### 【变式2】（23-24高一下·上海·课后作业）$\sin \frac{7}{12} \pi$的值是（ ）
$\quad$A． 
$\quad$B．$\frac{1}{4}$ 
$\quad$C．$\frac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4}$ 
$\quad$D．$\frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>D
<span class="fake-tag">分析</span>将非特殊角$\frac{7}{12} \pi$转化为特殊角$\frac{\pi}{4}$与$\frac{\pi}{3}$的和，然后利用两角和的正弦公式即可求解.
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>解：$\sin \frac{7}{12} \pi = \sin \left( \frac{\pi}{4} + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) = \sin \frac{\pi}{4} \cos \frac{\pi}{3} + \cos \frac{\pi}{4} \sin \frac{\pi}{3}$
$= \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$.
故选：D.
