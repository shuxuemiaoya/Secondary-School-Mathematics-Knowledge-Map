##### 【典例1】（2023下·四川眉山·高一校考期中）已知向量$\overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b}$满足$2 \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} = ( 0 , 3 )$，$\overrightarrow{a} - 2 \overrightarrow{b} = ( - 3 , 0 )$，$\lambda \overrightarrow{a} + \mu \overrightarrow{b} = ( - 1 , 1 )$，则$\lambda + \mu =$（ ）
$\quad$A．-1 
$\quad$B．0 
$\quad$C．1 
$\quad$D．$25$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>B
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>设$\overrightarrow{a} = \left( x_{1} , y_{1} \right)$，$\overrightarrow{b} = \left( x_{2} , y_{2} \right)$，又$2 \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} = ( 0 , 3 )$，$\overrightarrow{a} - 2 \overrightarrow{b} = ( - 3 , 0 )$，
所以$\begin{cases}2 x_{1} - x_{2} = 0 \\ 2 y_{1} - y_{2} = 3\end{cases}$，且$\begin{cases}x_{1} - 2 x_{2} = - 3 \\ y_{1} - 2 y_{2} = 0\end{cases}$，
解得$\begin{cases}x_{1} = 1 \\ y_{1} = 2\end{cases}$，$\begin{cases}x_{2} = 2 \\ y_{2} = 1\end{cases}$，即$\overrightarrow{a} = ( 1 , 2 )$，$\overrightarrow{b} = ( 2 , 1 )$.所以$\lambda \overrightarrow{a} + \mu \overrightarrow{b} = \lambda ( 1 , 2 ) + \mu ( 2 , 1 ) = ( \lambda + 2 \mu , 2 \lambda + \mu ) = ( - 1 , 1 )$，则$\begin{cases}\lambda + 2 \mu = - 1 \\ 2 \lambda + \mu = 1\end{cases}$，解得$\begin{cases}\lambda = 1 \\ \mu = - 1\end{cases}$，故$\lambda + \mu = 0$.
故选：B.
