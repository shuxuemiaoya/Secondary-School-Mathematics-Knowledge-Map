##### 题型09 向量的投影
##### 【典例1】（2023上·陕西西安·高二高新一中校考阶段练习）已知向量$\overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b}$不共线，满足$| \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} | = | \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} |$，则$\overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b}$在$\overrightarrow{b}$方向上的投影向量为（ ）
$\quad$A．$\overrightarrow{a}$ 
$\quad$B．$\overrightarrow{b}$ 
$\quad$C．$- \overrightarrow{a}$ 
$\quad$D．$- \overrightarrow{b}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>D
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$| \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} | = | \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} |$，
所以$\left| \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} = \left| \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2}$，即$\overrightarrow{a}^{2} + 2 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} + \overrightarrow{b}^{2} = \overrightarrow{a}^{2} - 2 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} + \overrightarrow{b}^{2}$，得$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 0$，
则$\overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b}$在$\overrightarrow{b}$方向上的投影向量为$\frac{\left( \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right) \cdot \overrightarrow{b}}{\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2}} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = \frac{\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} - \overrightarrow{b}^{2}}{\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2}} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = \frac{- \overrightarrow{b}^{2}}{\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2}} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = - \overrightarrow{b}$.
故选：D
##### 【典例2】（2023上·江苏无锡·高三校联考阶段练习）已知向量$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| = 2$，$\overrightarrow{b}$在$\overrightarrow{a}$方向上的投影向量为$- 3 \overrightarrow{a}$，则$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} =$ .
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$- 12$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$\overrightarrow{b}$在$\overrightarrow{a}$方向上的投影向量为$- 3 \overrightarrow{a}$，$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| = 2$，
所以$\frac{\overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{a}}{\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|} \cdot \frac{\overrightarrow{a}}{\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|} = - 3 \overrightarrow{a}$，即$\frac{\overrightarrow{b} \cdot \overrightarrow{a}}{4} \cdot \overrightarrow{a} = - 3 \overrightarrow{a}$，所以$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = - 12$.
故答案为：$- 12$.

##### 【变式1】（2023上·浙江·高二校联考期中）已知向量$\overrightarrow{a}$与单位向量$\overrightarrow{b}$的夹角为$\frac{\pi}{3}$，且$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| = 2$，则$\overrightarrow{b}$在$\overrightarrow{a}$方向上的投影向量为（ ）
$\quad$A．$\frac{1}{2}$ 
$\quad$B．$\frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{b}$ 
$\quad$C．$\frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{a}$ 
$\quad$D．$\frac{1}{4} \overrightarrow{a}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>D
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>$\overrightarrow{b}$在$\overrightarrow{a}$方向上的投影向量为$\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| \cos \left\langle \overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b} \right\rangle \cdot \frac{\overrightarrow{a}}{\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|} = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{a} = \frac{1}{4} \overrightarrow{a}$，
故选：D.

所以$\left( 2 \overrightarrow{b} - \overrightarrow{a} \right) \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 2 \overrightarrow{b}^{2} - \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 2 - ( - 1 ) = 3$，

##### 【变式3】（2023·贵州六盘水·统考模拟预测）已知$\overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b}$是相互垂直的单位向量.若向量$\overrightarrow{O A} = \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b}$，$\overrightarrow{O B} = 2 \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b}$，则向量在向量$\overrightarrow{A B}$上的投影向量为（ ）
$\quad$A．$\frac{1}{5} \overrightarrow{a} + \frac{2}{5} \overrightarrow{b}$ 
$\quad$B．$- \frac{1}{5} \overrightarrow{a} - \frac{2}{5} \overrightarrow{b}$
$\quad$C．$\frac{2}{5} \overrightarrow{a} + \frac{1}{5} \overrightarrow{b}$ 
$\quad$D．$- \frac{2}{5} \overrightarrow{a} - \frac{1}{5} \overrightarrow{b}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>B
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$\overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b}$是相互垂直的单位向量，
所以$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 0$，$\overrightarrow{a}^{2} = \overrightarrow{b}^{2} = 1$.
又$\overrightarrow{O A} = \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b}$，$\overrightarrow{O B} = 2 \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b}$，所以$\overrightarrow{A B} = \overrightarrow{O B} - \overrightarrow{O A} = \overrightarrow{a} + 2 \overrightarrow{b}$，
所以$\left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right| = \sqrt{\left( \overrightarrow{a} + 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right)^{2}} = \sqrt{\overrightarrow{a}^{2} + 4 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} + 4 \overrightarrow{b}^{2}} = \sqrt{5}$，
又$\overrightarrow{O A} \cdot \overrightarrow{A B} = \left( \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right) \cdot \left( \overrightarrow{a} + 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right) = \overrightarrow{a}^{2} + \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} - 2 \overrightarrow{b}^{2} = - 1$，
所以向量在向量$\overrightarrow{A B}$上的投影向量为$\frac{\overrightarrow{O A} \cdot \overrightarrow{A B}}{\left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right|} \cdot \frac{\overrightarrow{A B}}{\left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right|} = \frac{\overrightarrow{O A} \cdot \overrightarrow{A B}}{\left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right|^{2}} \overrightarrow{A B} = - \frac{1}{5} \left( \overrightarrow{a} + 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right) = - \frac{1}{5} \overrightarrow{a} - \frac{2}{5} \overrightarrow{b}$.
故选：B
