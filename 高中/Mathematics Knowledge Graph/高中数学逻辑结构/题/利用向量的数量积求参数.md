##### 【典例1】（2023下·四川巴中·高一统考期中）已知向量$\overrightarrow{a} = ( - 2 , m )$，$\overrightarrow{b} = ( 1 , 2 )$，$\left| \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} \right| = \left| \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right|$，则实数$m$的值为（ ）
$\quad$A． 
$\quad$B．$- \frac{1}{2}$ 
$\quad$C．$\frac{1}{2}$ 
$\quad$D．$1$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>D
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>$\because \left| \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} | = | \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right|$，
$\therefore \left( \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} \right)^{2} = \left( \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right)^{2}$，
即$\overrightarrow{a}^{2} + 2 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} + \overrightarrow{b}^{2} = \overrightarrow{a}^{2} - 2 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} + \overrightarrow{b}^{2}$，解得$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 0$，
$\therefore \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = - 2 + 2 m = 0$，解得$m = 1$．
故选：D
##### 【典例2】（2023下·广西河池·高二校联考阶段练习）已知平面向量$\overrightarrow{a} = ( m , 2 ) , \overrightarrow{b} = ( 1 , m ) , \left( \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} \right) \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = | \overrightarrow{b} |^{2}$，则实数$m =$ ．
