<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$\left\lbrack - 8 \sqrt{2} + 8 , 8 \sqrt{2} + 8 \right\rbrack$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>以正方形$A B C D$的中心$O$为原点建立平面直角坐标系如图所示，
则点$A ( - 2 , - 2 )$，$B ( 2 , - 2 )$，$\overrightarrow{A B} = ( 4 , 0 )$，
设以$x$轴非负半轴为始边，$O M$为终边的角为$( 0 \leq \alpha < 2 \pi )$，
易知外接圆的半径为$2 \sqrt{2}$，
所以点$M \left( 2 \sqrt{2} \cos \alpha , 2 \sqrt{2} \sin \alpha \right)$，则$\overrightarrow{A M} = \left( 2 \sqrt{2} \cos \alpha + 2 , 2 \sqrt{2} \sin \alpha + 2 \right)$，
所以$\overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A M} = \left( 2 \sqrt{2} \cos \alpha + 2 \right) \times 4 + \left( 2 \sqrt{2} \sin \alpha + 2 \right) \times 0 = 8 \sqrt{2} \cos \alpha + 8$，
因为$\cos \alpha \in [ - 1 , 1 ]$，所以$8 \sqrt{2} \cos \alpha + 8 \in \left\lbrack - 8 \sqrt{2} + 8 , 8 \sqrt{2} + 8 \right\rbrack$.
即$\overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A M}$的取值范围为$\left\lbrack - 8 \sqrt{2} + 8 , 8 \sqrt{2} + 8 \right\rbrack$.
故答案为：$\left\lbrack - 8 \sqrt{2} + 8 , 8 \sqrt{2} + 8 \right\rbrack$

##### 【变式1】（2023上·上海普陀·高三上海市晋元高级中学校考期中）已知点$M$是边长为2的正$\bigtriangleup A B C$内一点，且，若$\lambda + \mu = \frac{1}{2}$，则$\overrightarrow{M B} \cdot \overrightarrow{M C}$的最小值为（ ）.
$\quad$A．$- \frac{1}{3}$ 
$\quad$B． 
$\quad$C．$- \frac{1}{4}$ 
$\quad$D．$\frac{1}{4}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>C
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>取$B C$的中点$O$，以点$O$为坐标原点，$O C$、$O A$所在直线分别为$x$、$y$轴建立平面直角坐标系$x O y$，
则点$A \left( 0 , \sqrt{3} \right)$、$B ( - 1 , 0 )$、$C ( 1 , 0 )$，
![@@@4e51d417-3788-483f-b9d3-524e577c062a](MathLogic-Weave/高中数学逻辑结构/题/Attachments/81daf3188716bb09088f8d5c683d7bbd.png)
设点$M ( x , y )$，$\overrightarrow{A M} = \left( x , y - \sqrt{3} \right)$，$\overrightarrow{A B} = \left( - 1 , - \sqrt{3} \right)$，$\overrightarrow{A C} = \left( 1 , - \sqrt{3} \right)$，
$\because \overrightarrow{A M} = \lambda \overrightarrow{A B} + \mu \overrightarrow{A C}$且$\lambda + \mu = \frac{1}{2}$，则$\begin{cases}x = - \lambda + \mu \\ y - \sqrt{3} = - \sqrt{3} ( \lambda + \mu ) \\ \lambda + \mu = \frac{1}{2}\end{cases}$，可得$\begin{cases}x = \frac{1}{2} - 2 \lambda \\ y = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\end{cases}$，
由于点$M$在正$\bigtriangleup A B C$内，则$\begin{cases}\lambda > 0 \\ \mu > 0\end{cases}$，可得$0 < \lambda < \frac{1}{4}$，则$x \in \left( - \frac{1}{2} , \frac{1}{2} \right)$，
可得$\overrightarrow{M B} = \left( - 1 - x , - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right)$，$\overrightarrow{M C} = \left( 1 - x , - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \right)$，
$\therefore \overrightarrow{M B} \cdot \overrightarrow{M C} = x^{2} - 1 + \frac{3}{4} = x^{2} - \frac{1}{4}$，
所以当$x = 0$时，$\overrightarrow{M B} \cdot \overrightarrow{M C}$取最小值$- \frac{1}{4}$.
故选：C.
##### 【变式2】（2023·全国·模拟预测）如图，等腰梯形*ABCD*中，$A B = 4$，$A D = D C = B C = 2$，点*E*是线段*BD*上的动点，则$\overrightarrow{A E} \cdot \overrightarrow{C E}$的最小值为（ ）
![@@@b6423d7f-c228-4df8-a54f-8e9b452dcc82](MathLogic-Weave/高中数学逻辑结构/题/Attachments/741746a1445f13290fdcec4c943b6fdc.png)
$\quad$A．$- \frac{11}{4}$ 
$\quad$B．$\frac{11}{4}$ 
$\quad$C．$- \frac{27}{8}$ 
$\quad$D．$\frac{27}{8}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>A
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>如图，以*A*为原点，*AB*所在直线为*x*轴，过*A*且与*AB*垂直的直线为*y*轴建立平面直角坐标系，则$A ( 0 , 0 )$，$D \left( 1 , \sqrt{3} \right)$，$C \left( 3 , \sqrt{3} \right)$，$B ( 4 , 0 )$，所以$\overrightarrow{B D} = \left( - 3 , \sqrt{3} \right)$．
![@@@dbb0b370-b4fd-4c02-9d1e-951572bafa62](MathLogic-Weave/高中数学逻辑结构/题/Attachments/9e661f5a6792d9df1f8c48e567ccd23e.png)
设$E ( x , y )$，$\overrightarrow{B E} = \lambda \overrightarrow{B D}$，$\lambda \in [ 0 , 1 ]$，（注意判断$\lambda$的取值范围，为后续计算做准备）
则$( x - 4 , y ) = \lambda \left( - 3 , \sqrt{3} \right)$，所以$\begin{cases}x - 4 = - 3 \lambda \\ y = \sqrt{3} \lambda\end{cases}$，得$\begin{cases}x = 4 - 3 \lambda \\ y = \sqrt{3} \lambda\end{cases}$，
所以$E \left( 4 - 3 \lambda , \sqrt{3} \lambda \right)$，所以$\overrightarrow{A E} = \left( 4 - 3 \lambda , \sqrt{3} \lambda \right)$，$\overrightarrow{C E} = \left( 1 - 3 \lambda , \sqrt{3} \lambda - \sqrt{3} \right)$．
所以$\overrightarrow{A E} \cdot \overrightarrow{C E} = ( 4 - 3 \lambda ) ( 1 - 3 \lambda ) + \sqrt{3} \lambda \left( \sqrt{3} \lambda - \sqrt{3} \right) = 1 2 \lambda^{2} - 1 8 \lambda + 4 = 1 2 \times \left( \lambda - \frac{3}{4} \right)^{2} - \frac{11}{4}$，
所以当$\lambda = \frac{3}{4}$时，$\overrightarrow{A E} \cdot \overrightarrow{C E}$取得最小值，为$- \frac{11}{4}$．
故选：A
