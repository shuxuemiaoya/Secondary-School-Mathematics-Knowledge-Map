又因为$y = t , y = - \frac{1}{t}$在$\left\lbrack \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} , \sqrt{2} \right\rbrack$都为增函数，故$y = 2 \left( t - \frac{1}{t} \right)$在$\left\lbrack \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} , \sqrt{2} \right\rbrack$为增函数，
所以$y = k$与$y = 2 \left( t - \frac{1}{t} \right)$最多只有一个交点.
因为函数$h ( x ) = g ( x ) - k \left( \text{sin} x + \text{cos} x \right)$有两个不同的零点，
故$y = t$与$y = \sqrt{2} \text{sin} \left( x + \frac{\pi}{4} \right)$有两个不同的交点.
所以$t \in \left. \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2} , \sqrt{2} \right)$.
故$y = 2 \left( t - \frac{1}{t} \right) \in \left. \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3} , \sqrt{2} \right)$，
所以实数$k$的取值范围$\left. \frac{\sqrt{6}}{3} , \sqrt{2} \right)$.
