##### 【典例1】（2023·四川成都·石室中学校考模拟预测）已知平面向量$| \overrightarrow{a} | = 2$，$| \overrightarrow{b} | = 1$，$\overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b}$的夹角为$6 0^{\circ}$，$\left| \overrightarrow{a} + t \overrightarrow{b} \right| = \sqrt{3} \left( t \in \mathbf{R} \right)$，则实数$t$（ ）
$\quad$A． 
$\quad$B．1 
$\quad$C．$\frac{1}{2}$ 
$\quad$D．
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>A
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$\left| \overrightarrow{a} + t \overrightarrow{b} \right| = \sqrt{3}$，所以$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|^{2} + 2 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} \cdot t + t^{2} \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} = 3$，
即$4 + 2 \times 2 \times \cos 6 0^{\circ} t + t^{2} = 3$，解得$t = - 1$.
故选：A.
##### 【典例2】（2022·福建·高三专题练习）已知$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| = 2 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|$，$\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| \neq 0$，且关于$x$的方程$x^{2} + \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| x + \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 0$有实根，则$\overrightarrow{a}$与$\overrightarrow{b}$的夹角$\theta$的取值范围是（ ）
$\quad$A．$\left\lbrack 0 , \frac{\pi}{6} \right\rbrack$ 
$\quad$B．$\left\lbrack \frac{\pi}{3} , \pi \right\rbrack$
$\quad$C．$\left\lbrack \frac{\pi}{3} , \frac{2 \pi}{3} \right\rbrack$ 
$\quad$D．$\left\lbrack \frac{\pi}{6} , \pi \right\rbrack$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>B
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为关于$x$的方程$x^{2} + \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| x + \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 0$有实根，
所以$\Delta = \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|^{2} - 4 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|^{2} - 4 \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| \cdot \cos \theta = 4 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} - 8 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} \cdot \cos \theta \geq 0$，
所以$\cos \theta \leq \frac{1}{2}$，$\theta \in [ 0 , \pi ]$，
所以$\frac{\pi}{3} \leq \theta \leq \pi$，
即$\overrightarrow{a}$与$\overrightarrow{b}$的夹角$\theta$的取值范围是$\left\lbrack \frac{\pi}{3} , \pi \right\rbrack$.
故选：B.
##### 【变式1】（2023下·广东揭阳·高一校联考期中）已知向量$\overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b}$，若$| \overrightarrow{a} | = | \overrightarrow{b} | = 1 , \overrightarrow{a}$与$\overrightarrow{b}$的夹角为$6 0^{\circ}$；若$\overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b}$与$\overrightarrow{t a} - \overrightarrow{b}$的夹角为钝角，则$t$取值范围为（ ）
$\quad$A． 
$\quad$B．$( 1 , + \infty )$
$\quad$C．$( - 1 , 1 ) \cup ( 1 , + \infty )$ 
$\quad$D．$( - \infty , - 1 ) \cup ( - 1 , 1 )$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>D
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>$\because \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b}$与$\overrightarrow{t a} - \overrightarrow{b}$的夹角为钝角，
$\therefore \left( \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} \right) \cdot \left( \overrightarrow{t a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right) = t \overrightarrow{a}^{2} - \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} + t \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} - \overrightarrow{b}^{2} < 0$，
又$| \overrightarrow{a} | = | \overrightarrow{b} | = 1 , \overrightarrow{a}$与$\overrightarrow{b}$的夹角为$6 0^{\circ}$，
所以$t \overrightarrow{a}^{2} - \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} + t \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} - \overrightarrow{b}^{2} = t - \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} t - 1 < 0$，即$\frac{3}{2} t - \frac{3}{2} < 0$，解得$t < 1$，
又$\overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b}$与$\overrightarrow{t a} - \overrightarrow{b}$不共线，所以$t \neq - 1$，
所以$t$取值范围为$( - \infty , - 1 ) \cup ( - 1 , 1 )$.
故选：D
