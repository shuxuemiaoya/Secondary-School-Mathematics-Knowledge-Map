##### 题型04 已知数量积求模
##### 【典例1】（2023·四川凉山·统考一模）已知平面向量$\overrightarrow{a}$，$\overrightarrow{b}$满足$\left| \overrightarrow{a} - 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right| = 1$，$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 1$，则$\left| \overrightarrow{a} + 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right| =$（ ）
$\quad$A． 
$\quad$B．$2 \sqrt{2}$ 
$\quad$C．3 
$\quad$D．$2 \sqrt{3}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>C
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$\left| \overrightarrow{a} - 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right| = 1$，$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 1$，
所以$\left| \overrightarrow{a} + 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} = \overrightarrow{a}^{2} + 4 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} + 4 \overrightarrow{b}^{2} = \overrightarrow{a}^{2} - 4 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} + 4 \overrightarrow{b}^{2} + 8 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = \left| \overrightarrow{a} - 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} + 8 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 9$，
即$\left| \overrightarrow{a} + 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right| = 3$.
故选：C

##### 【变式1】（2023上·湖北·高二湖北省红安县第一中学校联考阶段练习）已知$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 1$，$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| = 2$，$\overrightarrow{a}$，$\overrightarrow{b}$的夹角为$\frac{\pi}{3}$，则$\left| \overrightarrow{a} - 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right| =$（ ）
$\quad$A．1 
$\quad$B．$\sqrt{2}$ 
$\quad$C．2 
$\quad$D．4
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>C
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 1$，$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| = 2$，$\overrightarrow{a}$，$\overrightarrow{b}$的夹角为$\frac{\pi}{3}$，
所以$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| \cos \left\langle \overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b} \right\rangle = 2 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| \cos \frac{\pi}{3} = 1$，
解得$\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| = 1$，
$\left| \overrightarrow{a} - 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right| = \sqrt{\left( \overrightarrow{a} - 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right)^{2}} = \sqrt{\overrightarrow{a}^{2} - 4 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} + 4 \overrightarrow{b}^{2}} = \sqrt{4 - 4 \times 1 + 4 \times 1^{2}} = 2$，
故选：C.
