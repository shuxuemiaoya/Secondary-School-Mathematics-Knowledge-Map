##### 题型03 $\mathbf{1} \mathbf{5}^{\mathbf{\circ}}$（$\mathbf{7} \mathbf{5}^{\mathbf{\circ}}$）的余弦值
##### 【典例1】（23-24高一下·山东临沂·期中）（ ）
$\quad$A． 
$\quad$B．$\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{3}$ 
$\quad$C．$\frac{\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{2}}{4}$ 
$\quad$D．$\frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>D
<span class="fake-tag">分析</span>根据$\text{cos} 15 {^\circ} = \cos ( 45 {^\circ} - 30 {^\circ} )$结合两角差的余弦公式运算求解.
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>由题意可得：$\text{cos} 15 {^\circ} = \cos ( 45 {^\circ} - 30 {^\circ} ) = \cos 4 5 {^\circ} \cos 3 0 {^\circ} + \sin 4 5 {^\circ} \sin 3 0 {^\circ}$
$= \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$，
所以$\text{cos} 15 {^\circ} = \frac{\sqrt{6} + \sqrt{2}}{4}$.
故选：D.

##### 【典例3】（23-24高一·全国·随堂练习）求下列各式的值：
(1)$\cos 1 05 {^\circ}$；
(2)$\cos \left( - \frac{2 5 \pi}{12} \right)$．
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>(1)$\frac{\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{6}}{4}$；
(2)$\frac{\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{6}}{4}$.
<span class="fake-tag">分析</span>（1）$105 {^\circ} = 6 0 {^\circ} + 4 5 {^\circ}$，由两角和的余弦公式即可求解；
（2）由诱导公式可得$\cos \left( - \frac{2 5 \pi}{12} \right) = \cos \frac{\pi}{12}$，$\frac{\pi}{12} = \frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{\pi}{4}$，由两角差的余弦公式即可求解.
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>（1）$\cos 1 05 {^\circ} = \cos ( 60 {^\circ} + 4 5 {^\circ} ) = \cos 6 0 {^\circ} \cos 4 5 {^\circ} - \sin 6 0 {^\circ} \sin 4 5 {^\circ}$
$= \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{6}}{4}$.
（2）$\cos \left( - \frac{2 5 \pi}{12} \right) = \cos \frac{2 5 \pi}{12} = \cos \left( 2 \pi + \frac{\pi}{12} \right) = \cos \frac{\pi}{12}$

$= \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{6}}{4}$.
