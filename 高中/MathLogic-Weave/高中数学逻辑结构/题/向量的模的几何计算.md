(2)$4$

##### 【典例2】（2023·高一课时练习）已知向量$\overrightarrow{a}$，$\overrightarrow{b}$满足$| \overrightarrow{a} | = 3$，$| \overrightarrow{b} | = 4$，则$| \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} |$的最大值为 ．
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>7
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$| \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} | \leq \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| + \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| = 7$，当且仅当$\overrightarrow{a}$，$\overrightarrow{b}$反向时，等号成立，
所以$| \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} |$的最大值为7.
故答案为：7.
##### 【变式1】（2022·高一课时练习）已知菱形*ABCD*的边长为1.且$\angle A = 1 2 0 {^\circ}$，求$\left| \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{B C} - \overrightarrow{C D} \right|$的值.
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$| \overrightarrow{A C} | = | \overrightarrow{A B} | = 1$
所以$\left| \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{B C} - \overrightarrow{C D} \right| = \left| \overrightarrow{A C} - \overrightarrow{B A} \right| = \left| \overrightarrow{A C} + \overrightarrow{A B} \right| = \sqrt{\overrightarrow{A C}^{2} + \overrightarrow{A B}^{2} + 2 \overrightarrow{A C} \cdot \overrightarrow{A B}}$
= $\sqrt{1 + 1 + 2 \times 1 \times 1 \times \frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{3}$
