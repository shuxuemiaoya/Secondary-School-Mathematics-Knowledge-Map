##### 题型06 利用$\mathbf{\sin} \mathbf{\alpha} \mathbf{+} \mathbf{\cos} \mathbf{\alpha}$,$\mathbf{\sin} \mathbf{\alpha} \mathbf{-} \mathbf{\cos} \mathbf{\alpha}$与$\mathbf{\sin} \mathbf{\alpha} \mathbf{\cos} \mathbf{\alpha}$之间的关系求值
##### 【典例1】（23-24高一下·辽宁沈阳·阶段练习）已知$\theta \in ( 0 , \pi ) ， \text{sin} \theta + \text{cos} \theta = - \frac{1}{5}$，则下列结论不正确的是（ ）
$\quad$A．$\theta \in \left( \frac{\pi}{2} , \pi \right)$ 
$\quad$B．$\text{cos} \theta = - \frac{3}{5}$
$\quad$C．$\text{tan} \theta = - \frac{3}{4}$ 
$\quad$D．$\text{sin} \theta - \text{cos} \theta = \frac{7}{5}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>B
<span class="fake-tag">分析</span>根据给定条件，求得$\sin \theta \cos \theta , \sin \theta - \cos \theta$，再逐项分析判断得解.
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>由$\theta \in ( 0 , \pi ) ， \text{sin} \theta + \text{cos} \theta = - \frac{1}{5}$，得$1 + 2 \sin \theta \cos \theta = \frac{1}{25}$，解得$2 \sin \theta \cos \theta = - \frac{24}{25} < 0$，
对于A，$\sin \theta > 0$，则$\cos \theta < 0$，$\theta \in ( \frac{\pi}{2} , \pi )$，A正确；
对于D，$\text{sin} \theta - \text{cos} \theta = \sqrt{( \text{sin} \theta - \text{cos} \theta )^{2}} = \sqrt{1 - 2 \sin \theta \cos \theta} = \frac{7}{5}$，D正确；
对于B，由$\sin \theta + \cos \theta = - \frac{1}{5}$，$\text{sin} \theta - \text{cos} \theta = \frac{7}{5}$，得$\sin \theta = \frac{3}{5} , \cos \theta = - \frac{4}{5}$，B错误；
对于C，$\text{tan} \theta = \frac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} = - \frac{3}{4}$，C正确.
故选：B
