##### 【典例1】（2023上·广东深圳·高二校考阶段练习）人脸识别技术应用在各行各业，改变着人类的生活，而所谓人脸识别，就是利用计算机分析人脸视频或者图像，并从中提取出有效的识别信息，最终判别人脸对象的身份.在人脸识别中为了检测样本之间的相似度主要应用距离的测试，常用的测量距离的方式有曼哈顿距离和余弦距离.假设二维空间中有两个点$A \left( x_{1} , y_{1} \right)$、$B \left( x_{2} , y_{2} \right)$，$O$为坐标原点，余弦相似度为向量、$\overrightarrow{O B}$夹角的余弦值，记作$\cos ( A , B )$，余弦距离为.已知$P \left( \cos \alpha , \sin \alpha \right)$、$Q \left( \cos \beta , \sin \beta \right)$、$R \left( \cos \alpha , - \sin \alpha \right)$，若$P$、$Q$的余弦距离为，$\tan \alpha \cdot \tan \beta = \frac{1}{7}$，则$Q$、的余弦距离为（ ）
$\quad$A．$\frac{1}{2}$ 
$\quad$B． 
$\quad$C．$\frac{1}{4}$ 
$\quad$D．$\frac{1}{7}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>A
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>由$\overrightarrow{O P} = \left( \cos \alpha , \sin \alpha \right)$，$\overrightarrow{O Q} = \left( \cos \beta , \sin \beta \right)$，$\overrightarrow{O R} = \left( \cos \alpha , - \sin \alpha \right)$，
$\cos ( P , Q ) = \frac{\overrightarrow{O P} \cdot \overrightarrow{O Q}}{\left| \overrightarrow{O P} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow{O Q} \right|} = \sin \alpha \sin \beta + \cos \alpha \cos \beta = \cos ( \alpha - \beta )$，
$\cos ( Q , R ) = \frac{\overrightarrow{O Q} \cdot \overrightarrow{O R}}{\left| \overrightarrow{O Q} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow{O R} \right|} = \cos \alpha \cos \beta - \sin \alpha \sin \beta = \cos ( \alpha + \beta )$，
由已知$1 - \cos ( \alpha - \beta ) = \frac{1}{3}$，可得$\cos ( \alpha - \beta ) = \cos \alpha \cos \beta + \sin \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{2}{3}$，①
又因为$\tan \alpha \tan \beta = \frac{\sin \alpha \sin \beta}{\cos \alpha \cos \beta} = \frac{1}{7}$，②
联立①②可得$\sin \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{1}{12}$，$\cos \alpha \cos \beta = \frac{7}{12}$，
因此，$Q$、的余弦距离为$1 - \cos ( \alpha + \beta ) = 1 - \cos \alpha \cos \beta + \sin \alpha \sin \beta = 1 - \frac{7}{12} + \frac{1}{12} = \frac{1}{2}$，
故选：A.

<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>依题意，$\overrightarrow{B E} = 4 \overrightarrow{B D}$，即$\overrightarrow{A E} - \overrightarrow{A B} = 4 ( \overrightarrow{A D} - \overrightarrow{A B} )$，则$\overrightarrow{A D} = \frac{3}{4} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{4} \overrightarrow{A E}$，同理$\overrightarrow{A E} = \frac{3}{4} \overrightarrow{A C} + \frac{1}{4} \overrightarrow{A F}$，
因此$\overrightarrow{A D} = \frac{3}{4} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{4} \overrightarrow{A E} = \frac{3}{4} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{4} ( \frac{3}{4} \overrightarrow{A C} + \frac{1}{4} \overrightarrow{A F} ) = \frac{3}{4} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{3}{16} \overrightarrow{A C} + \frac{1}{16} \cdot \frac{1}{4} \overrightarrow{A D}$，
即$\frac{63}{64} \overrightarrow{A D} = \frac{3}{4} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{3}{16} \overrightarrow{A C}$，整理得$\overrightarrow{A D} = \frac{16}{21} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{4}{21} \overrightarrow{A C}$，而$\overrightarrow{A D} = \lambda \overrightarrow{A B} + \mu \overrightarrow{A C}$，且$\overrightarrow{A B} , \overrightarrow{A C}$不共线，
于是$\lambda = \frac{16}{21} , \mu = \frac{4}{21}$，所以$\lambda ＋ 2 \mu = \frac{16}{21} + 2 \times \frac{4}{21} = \frac{8}{7}$.
