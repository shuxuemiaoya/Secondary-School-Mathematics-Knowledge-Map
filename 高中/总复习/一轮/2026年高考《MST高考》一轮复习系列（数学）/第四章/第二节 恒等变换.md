# 题型 2 二倍角公式的应用

2. 二倍角公式

① $\sin 2\alpha = 2\sin \alpha \cos \alpha$ ; ② $\cos 2\alpha = \cos^{2}\alpha - \sin^{2}\alpha = 2\cos^{2}\alpha - 1 = 1 - 2\sin^{2}\alpha$ ; ③ $\tan 2\alpha = \frac{2\tan\alpha}{1 - \tan^{2}\alpha}$ ;

其他常用变式

$$
\sin 2 \alpha = \frac {2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {2 \tan \alpha}{1 + \tan^ {2} \alpha}; \quad \cos 2 \alpha = \frac {\cos^ {2} \alpha - \sin^ {2} \alpha}{\sin^ {2} \alpha + \cos^ {2} \alpha} = \frac {1 - \tan^ {2} \alpha}{1 + \tan^ {2} \alpha}; \quad \tan \frac {\alpha}{2} = \frac {\sin \alpha}{1 + \cos \alpha} = \frac {1 - \cos \alpha}{\sin \alpha}.
$$

# 拓展 1 和差化积与积化和差

1. 和化积公式

$$
\sin \alpha + \sin \beta = 2 \sin \frac {\alpha + \beta}{2} \cos \frac {\alpha - \beta}{2} \quad \sin \alpha - \sin \beta = 2 \cos \frac {\alpha + \beta}{2} \sin \frac {\alpha - \beta}{2}
$$

$$
\cos \alpha + \cos \beta = 2 \cos \frac {\alpha + \beta}{2} \cos \frac {\alpha - \beta}{2} \quad \cos \alpha - \cos \beta = - 2 \sin \frac {\alpha + \beta}{2} \sin \frac {\alpha - \beta}{2}
$$

(由和差公式可得)

2. 积化和公式

$$
\sin \alpha \cdot \cos \beta = \frac {1}{2} [ \sin (\alpha + \beta) + \sin (\alpha - \beta) ]
$$

$$
\cos \alpha \cdot \cos \beta = \frac {1}{2} [ \cos (\alpha + \beta) + \cos (\alpha - \beta) ]
$$

$$
\sin \alpha \cdot \sin \beta = \frac {1}{2} [ \cos (\alpha - \beta) - \cos (\alpha + \beta) ]
$$

(由和差公式可得)
