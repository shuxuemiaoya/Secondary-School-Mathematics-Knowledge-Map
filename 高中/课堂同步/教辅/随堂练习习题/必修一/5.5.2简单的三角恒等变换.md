### 知识点01 ：半角公式
①$\mathbf{\sin} \frac{\mathbf{\alpha}}{\mathbf{2}} \mathbf{=} \mathbf{\pm} \sqrt{\frac{\mathbf{1} \mathbf{-} \mathbf{\cos} \mathbf{\alpha}}{\mathbf{2}}}$
②$\mathbf{\cos} \frac{\mathbf{\alpha}}{\mathbf{2}} \mathbf{=} \mathbf{\pm} \sqrt{\frac{\mathbf{1 +} \mathbf{\cos} \mathbf{\alpha}}{\mathbf{2}}}$
③$\mathbf{\tan} \frac{\mathbf{\alpha}}{\mathbf{2}} \mathbf{=} \mathbf{\pm} \sqrt{\frac{\mathbf{1} \mathbf{-} \mathbf{\cos} \mathbf{\alpha}}{\mathbf{1 +} \mathbf{\cos} \mathbf{\alpha}}} \mathbf{=} \frac{\mathbf{1} \mathbf{-} \mathbf{\cos} \mathbf{\alpha}}{\mathbf{\sin} \mathbf{\alpha}} \mathbf{=} \frac{\mathbf{\sin} \mathbf{\alpha}}{\mathbf{1 +} \mathbf{\cos} \mathbf{\alpha}}$
### 知识点02 ：辅助角公式：
$a \sin x \pm b \cos x = \sqrt{a^{2} + b^{2}} \sin ( x \pm \varphi )$ (其中$\tan \varphi = \frac{b}{a}$)
### 知识点03 ：万能公式
①$\sin \alpha = \frac{2 \tan \frac{\alpha}{2}}{1 + \tan^{2} \frac{\alpha}{2}}$
②$\cos \alpha = \frac{1 - \tan^{2} \frac{\alpha}{2}}{1 + \tan^{2} \frac{\alpha}{2}}$
③$\tan \alpha = \frac{2 \tan \frac{\alpha}{2}}{1 - \tan^{2} \frac{\alpha}{2}}$
