所以$\overrightarrow{B Q} = \left( 1 - \frac{\lambda}{5} \right) \overrightarrow{A C} - \overrightarrow{A B}$，$\overrightarrow{C P} = \lambda \overrightarrow{A B} - \overrightarrow{A C}$，
所以$\overrightarrow{B Q} \cdot \overrightarrow{C P} = \left\lbrack \left( 1 - \frac{\lambda}{5} \right) \overrightarrow{A C} - \overrightarrow{A B} \right\rbrack \cdot \left( \lambda \overrightarrow{A B} - \overrightarrow{A C} \right) = \left( \frac{\lambda}{5} - 1 \right) \overrightarrow{A C}^{2} - \lambda \overrightarrow{A B}^{2} + \left( 1 + \lambda - \frac{\lambda^{2}}{5} \right) \overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A C}$，
