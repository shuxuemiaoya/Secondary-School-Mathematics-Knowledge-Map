# Question 3

<!-- question-source:start -->
3. 设正实数 $x, y, z$ 满足 $x^{2} - 3xy + 4y^{2} - z = 0$ , 则当 $\frac{xy}{z}$ 取得最大值时, $\frac{2}{x} + \frac{1}{y} - \frac{2}{z}$ 的最大值为 ( )
A. 9 B. 1 C. $\frac{9}{4}$ D. 3
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
3. B 【解析】∵ $x \otimes (x - 2) = x(x - 2) + 2x + x - 2 < 0$ , ∴ $x^{2} + x - 2 < 0$ , 即 $(x - 1)(x + 2) < 0$ , 解得 -2 < x < 1. 故选 B.
