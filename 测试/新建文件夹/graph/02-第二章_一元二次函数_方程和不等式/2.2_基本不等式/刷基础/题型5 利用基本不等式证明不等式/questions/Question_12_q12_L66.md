# Question 12

<!-- question-source:start -->
12.[江西南昌2025高一月考]已知x>0,y>0,x+2y=1,证明: $\left(1+\frac{2}{x}\right)\left(1+\frac{1}{y}\right)\geqslant25.$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
12.【证明】∵ x > 0, y > 0, $x + 2y = 1$ , $\therefore\left(1+\frac{2}{x}\right)\left(1+\frac{1}{y}\right)=\left(1+\frac{2x+4y}{x}\right)\left(1+\frac{x+2y}{y}\right)=\left(3+\frac{4y}{x}\right)\left(3+\frac{x}{y}\right)=13+\left(\frac{12y}{x}+\frac{3x}{y}\right)\geqslant13+2\sqrt{\frac{12y}{x}\cdot\frac{3x}{y}}=25$ ，当
且仅当 $\frac{4y}{x}=\frac{x}{y}$ ，即 $y=\frac{1}{4},x=\frac{1}{2}$ 时，等号成立， $\therefore\left(1+\frac{2}{x}\right)\left(1+\frac{1}{y}\right)\geqslant25.$
