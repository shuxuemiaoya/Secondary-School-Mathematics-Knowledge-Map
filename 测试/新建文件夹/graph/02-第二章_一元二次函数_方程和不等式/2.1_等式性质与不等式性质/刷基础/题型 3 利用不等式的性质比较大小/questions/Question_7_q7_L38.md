# Question 7

<!-- question-source:start -->
7.[安徽部分学校2025高一联考]设 $p=m^{2}-m+$ 2, $q=\frac{1}{m^{2}+m+2}$ ，则p与4q的大小为\_\_\_\_。
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
7. $p \geqslant 4q$ 【解析】因为 $p = m^2 - m + 2 = \left(m - \frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \frac{7}{4} > 0, q = \frac{1}{m^2 + m + 2} = \frac{1}{\left(m + \frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \frac{7}{4}} > 0$ ，则 $\frac{p}{q} = (m^2 - m + 2)(m^2 + m + 2) = (m^2 + 2)^2 - m^2 = m^4 + 3m^2 + 4 \geqslant 4$ ，故 $p \geqslant 4q$ ，当且仅当 $m = 0$ 时取等号.
