# Question 6

<!-- question-source:start -->
6. 若 $\cos(x-20^{\circ})=2\cos x\sin10^{\circ}$ ，则 $\tan x=$ \_\_\_\_.
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
6. $-\sqrt{3}$ 【解析】由题可得 $\cos x\cos 20^{\circ} + \sin x\sin 20^{\circ} = 2\cos x\sin 10^{\circ}$ ，易知 $\cos x \neq 0$ ，将等式两边同时除以 $\cos x$ 可得 $\cos 20^{\circ} + \tan x\sin 20^{\circ} = 2\sin 10^{\circ}$ ，所以 $\tan x = \frac{2\sin 10^{\circ} - \cos 20^{\circ}}{\sin 20^{\circ}}$

$= \frac{2\sin(30^{\circ} - 20^{\circ}) - \cos 20^{\circ}}{\sin 20^{\circ}}$ $= \frac{2\sin 30^{\circ}\cos 20^{\circ} - 2\cos 30^{\circ}\sin 20^{\circ} - \cos 20^{\circ}}{\sin 20^{\circ}}$ $= -2\cos 30^{\circ} = -\sqrt{3}.$
<!-- answer-source:end -->
