# Question 8

<!-- question-source:start -->
8. 已知 $\sin \alpha = -\frac{12}{13}, \alpha \in \left(\pi, \frac{3\pi}{2}\right)$ , 则 $\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} =$ \_\_\_\_.
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
8. $-\frac{3}{2}$ 【解析】因为 $\sin \alpha = -\frac{12}{13}, \alpha \in (\pi, \frac{3\pi}{2})$ ，
所以 $\cos \alpha = -\frac{5}{13}$ .
因为 $\alpha \in (\pi, \frac{3\pi}{2})$ ，
所以 $\frac{\alpha}{2} \in \left(\frac{\pi}{2}, \frac{3\pi}{4}\right)$ ,
所以 $\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} < 0$ ,
所以 $\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} = -\sqrt{\frac{1 - \cos \alpha}{1 + \cos \alpha}} = -\frac{3}{2}$ .
→ 点悟： $\tan \frac{\alpha}{2} = -\sqrt{\frac{\sin^2 \frac{\alpha}{2}}{\cos^2 \frac{\alpha}{2}}} = -\sqrt{\frac{2\sin^2 \frac{\alpha}{2}}{2\cos^2 \frac{\alpha}{2}}} = -\sqrt{\frac{1 - \cos \alpha}{1 + \cos \alpha}}$
<!-- answer-source:end -->
