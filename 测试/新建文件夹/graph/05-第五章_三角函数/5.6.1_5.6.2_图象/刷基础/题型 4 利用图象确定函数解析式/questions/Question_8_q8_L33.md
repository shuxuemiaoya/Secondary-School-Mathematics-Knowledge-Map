# Question 8

<!-- question-source:start -->
8.[天津南开中学2025高一质量检测]函数 $f(x)=a\sin x+b(x\in[0,2\pi]$ 且 $a,b\in\mathbf{R}$ 的图象如图所示,则函数 $f(x)$ 的解析式为()

![](../../../../../images/questions/part-002/ee395710ef75190d6a4eea10ebc3a9ea180a97bfeb73e3acaa89f2598119a5b8.jpg)

A. $f(x) = \frac{1}{2}\sin x + 1,x\in [0,2\pi ]$ B. $f(x) = \sin x + \frac{1}{2},x\in [0,2\pi ]$ C. $f(x) = \frac{3}{2}\sin x + 1,x\in [0,2\pi ]$ D. $f(x) = \frac{3}{2}\sin x + \frac{1}{2},x\in [0,2\pi ]$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
8. A 【解析】把点(0,1)与点 $\left(\frac{\pi}{2},1.5\right)$ 的坐标代入 $f(x)=a\sin x+b$ 中，可得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}b&=1,\\ a+b&=1.5,\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a&=0.5,\\ b&=1,\end{aligned}\right.$ 因此 $f(x)=\frac{1}{2}\sin x+1,x\in[0,2\pi]$ ，故选A.
