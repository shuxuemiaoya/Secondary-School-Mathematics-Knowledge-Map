# Question 1

<!-- question-source:start -->
1. 用“五点法”作函数 $y = \cos \left(4x - \frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ 在一个周期内的图象时, 第四个关键点的坐标是 ( )
A. $\left(\frac{5\pi}{12},0\right)$ B. $\left(-\frac{5\pi}{12},1\right)$ C. $\left(\frac{5\pi}{12},1\right)$ D. $\left(-\frac{5\pi}{12},0\right)$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
1. A 【解析】令 $4x - \frac{\pi}{6} = \frac{3\pi}{2}$ ，得 $x = \frac{5\pi}{12}$ ，故第四个关键点的坐标为 $\left(\frac{5\pi}{12}, 0\right)$ 。故选 A.
<!-- answer-source:end -->
