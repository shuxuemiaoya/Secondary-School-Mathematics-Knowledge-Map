# Question 3

<!-- question-source:start -->
3.[江苏连云港2025高一期末]为了研究钟表与三角函数的关系,建立如图所示的坐标系,设秒针针尖位置为 $P(x,y)$ .若初始位置为 $P_{0}\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2},\frac{1}{2}\right)$ ,当秒针针尖从 $P_{0}$ (注:此时t=0)正常开始走时,点P的纵坐标y与时间t(单位:秒)的函数关系式为()

A. $y = \sin \left(\frac{\pi}{30} t + \frac{\pi}{6}\right)(t\geqslant 0)$ B. $y = \sin \left(-\frac{\pi}{60} t - \frac{\pi}{6}\right)(t\geqslant 0)$ C. $y = \sin \left(-\frac{\pi}{30} t + \frac{\pi}{6}\right)(t\geqslant 0)$ D. $y = \sin \left(-\frac{\pi}{30} t + \frac{\pi}{3}\right)(t\geqslant 0)$

![](../../../../images/questions/part-002/8260fb5f0959ec5f286778e97c28f2bb5b735e9c723d65b195d32fc967ed1474.jpg)
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
3. C 【解析】根据题意, 设 $y = A \sin(\omega t + \varphi)$ (A > 0, $\omega < 0$ , $-\pi \leqslant \varphi < \pi$ ), 由题意可知, 初相 $\varphi$ 为第一象限角, 且 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\cos\varphi &= \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \\ \sin\varphi &= \frac{1}{2},\end{aligned}\right.$ 又因为 $-\pi \leqslant \varphi < \pi$ , 则 $\varphi = \frac{\pi}{6}$ , $A = \sqrt{\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2} = 1$ ,

函数 $y = \sin\left(\omega t + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) (\omega < 0)$ 的最小正周期 T = 60,

所以 $\omega = -\frac{2\pi}{T} = -\frac{2\pi}{60} = -\frac{\pi}{30}$ ,

故黑板: 因为秒针按顺时针旋转, 所以 $\omega$ 为负值

所以点 P 的纵坐标 y 与时间 t 的函数关系式为 $y = \sin\left(-\frac{\pi}{30} t + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) (t \geqslant 0)$ . 故选 C.
