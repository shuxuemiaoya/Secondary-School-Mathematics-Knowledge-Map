# Question 12

<!-- question-source:start -->
12. 已知 $\cos(508^{\circ}-\alpha)=\frac{12}{13}$ ，则 $\cos(212^{\circ}+\alpha)=$ \_\_\_\_.
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
12. D 【解析】由题意 $\frac{\cos(-585^{\circ})}{\tan585^{\circ}+\sin570^{\circ}}$ $=\frac{\cos(135^{\circ}-360^{\circ}\times2)}{\tan(45^{\circ}+180^{\circ}\times3)+\sin(210^{\circ}+360^{\circ})}$ $=\frac{\cos135^{\circ}}{\tan45^{\circ}+\sin210^{\circ}}$ $=\frac{\cos(45^{\circ}+90^{\circ})}{\tan45^{\circ}+\sin(30^{\circ}+180^{\circ})}$ $=\frac{-\sin45^{\circ}}{\tan45^{\circ}-\sin30^{\circ}}$ $=\frac{-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}{1-\frac{1}{2}}=-\sqrt{2}$ ，故选 D.
