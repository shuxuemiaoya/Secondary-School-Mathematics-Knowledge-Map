# Question 10

<!-- question-source:start -->
10. $\sqrt{3}\sin 15^{\circ} + \sin 75^{\circ} =$ （）A. $\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ B. 1 C. $\sqrt{2}$ D. $\frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
10. C 【解析】 $\sqrt{3}\sin15^{\circ}+\sin75^{\circ}=\sqrt{3}\sin15^{\circ}+\cos15^{\circ}=2\sin(15^{\circ}+30^{\circ})=$ → 点悟： $\sqrt{3}\sin15^{\circ}+\cos15^{\circ}=2\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sin15^{\circ}+\frac{1}{2}\cos15^{\circ}\right)=2\left(\cos30^{\circ}\cdot\sin15^{\circ}+\sin30^{\circ}\cos15^{\circ}\right)$ $2\sin45^{\circ}=\sqrt{2}$ . 故选 C.
