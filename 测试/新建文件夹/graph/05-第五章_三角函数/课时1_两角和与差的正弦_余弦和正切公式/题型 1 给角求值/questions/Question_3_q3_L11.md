# Question 3

<!-- question-source:start -->
3.[湖南株洲二中2024高一期末]若 $\alpha +\beta = \frac{3\pi}{4}$ 则(1-tan $\alpha$ )(1-tan $\beta$ )A.0 B.1 C. $\sqrt{3}$ D.2
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
3. D 【解析】因为 $\alpha + \beta = \frac{3\pi}{4}$ ，所以 $\tan(\alpha + \beta) = \frac{\tan\alpha + \tan\beta}{1 - \tan\alpha\tan\beta} = \tan\frac{3\pi}{4} = -1$ ，
所以 $\tan\alpha+\tan\beta=\tan\alpha\tan\beta-1$ 所以 $(1-\tan\alpha)(1-\tan\beta)=1-(\tan\alpha+\tan\beta)+\tan\alpha\tan\beta=1-(\tan\alpha\tan\beta-1)+\tan\alpha\tan\beta=2.$ 故选 D.
