# Question 2

<!-- question-source:start -->
2. 已知某扇形的面积为 $2.5 \, cm^{2}$ ，若该扇形的半径 r，弧长 l 满足 $2r + l = 7 \, cm$ ，则该扇形圆心角的弧度数是（）
A. $\frac{4}{5}$ B.5
C. $\frac{1}{2}$ D. $\frac{4}{5}$ 或5
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
2. D 【解析】根据题意, 得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}l+2r=7,\\ \frac{1}{2}lr=2.5,\end{aligned}\right.$ 解得 $\left\{\begin{aligned}r=\frac{5}{2},\\ l=2\end{aligned}\right.$ 或 $\left\{\begin{aligned}r=1,\\ l=5,\end{aligned}\right.$ 所以 $\frac{l}{r}=\frac{4}{5}$ 或 5. 故选 D.
