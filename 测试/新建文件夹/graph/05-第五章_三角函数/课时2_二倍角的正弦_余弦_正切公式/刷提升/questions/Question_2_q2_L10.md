# Question 2

<!-- question-source:start -->
2.[江苏靖江高级中学2025段考]已知锐角 $\alpha, \beta$ 满足 $\sin \alpha + \sin \alpha \sin \beta = \cos \alpha \cos \beta$ , 则 $2 \alpha + \beta =$ ( )
A. $\frac{\pi}{4}$ B. $\frac{\pi}{3}$ C. $\frac{\pi}{2}$ D. $\pi$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
2. D 【解析】由 $\sin\theta=\frac{4}{5}$ ， $\theta$ 为第二象限角，
则 $\cos\theta=-\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^{2}}=-\frac{3}{5}$ ,
所以 $\cos^{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\frac{\theta}{2}\right)=\sin^{2}\frac{\theta}{2}=\frac{1-\cos\theta}{2}=$ $\frac{4}{5}$ . 故选 D.
