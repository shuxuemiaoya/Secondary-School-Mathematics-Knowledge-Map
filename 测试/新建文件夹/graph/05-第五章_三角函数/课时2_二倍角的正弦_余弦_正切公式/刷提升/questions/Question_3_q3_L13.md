# Question 3

<!-- question-source:start -->
3.[天津塘沽一中2025高一月考]若 $\cos (\alpha -\beta) =$ $\frac{\sqrt{5}}{5},\cos 2\alpha = \frac{\sqrt{10}}{10}$ 并且 $\alpha ,\beta$ 均为锐角，且 $\alpha <  \beta$ 则 $\alpha +\beta$ 的值为 （）A. $\frac{\pi}{6}$ B. $\frac{\pi}{4}$ C. $\frac{3\pi}{4}$ D. $\frac{5\pi}{6}$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
3.B 【解析】因为 $\alpha \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right), \beta \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right), \tan \alpha = \frac{\cos 2\beta}{1 - \sin 2\beta},$ 所以 $\tan \alpha = \frac{\cos^2\beta - \sin^2\beta}{\cos^2\beta + \sin^2\beta - 2\sin\beta\cos\beta} = \frac{(\cos\beta - \sin\beta)(\cos\beta + \sin\beta)}{(\cos\beta - \sin\beta)^2},$ 因为 $\cos \beta - \sin \beta \neq 0,$ 所以 $\tan \alpha = \frac{\cos\beta + \sin\beta}{\cos\beta - \sin\beta} = \frac{1 + \tan\beta}{1 - \tan\beta} = \tan \left(\frac{\pi}{4} + \beta\right).$ 因为 $\alpha \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right), \beta \in \left(0, \frac{\pi}{2}\right)$ ，所以 $\frac{\pi}{4} + \beta \in \left(\frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{3\pi}{4}\right)$ ，所以 $\alpha = \frac{\pi}{4} + \beta,$ 即 $\alpha - \beta = \frac{\pi}{4}$ . 故选B.
