# Question 9

<!-- question-source:start -->
9.(多选)[山东新泰一中2025质量检测]下列各式中,计算结果为 $\sqrt{3}$ 的是 ( )
A. $\cos 85^{\circ}\cos 25^{\circ}-\sin 85^{\circ}\sin 25^{\circ}$ B. $\tan 25^{\circ}+\tan 35^{\circ}+\sqrt{3}\tan 25^{\circ}\tan 35^{\circ}$ C. $\frac{\sin 15^{\circ}+\cos 15^{\circ}}{\cos 15^{\circ}-\sin 15^{\circ}}$ D. $\frac{\sin 40^{\circ}+\sin 80^{\circ}}{\cos 20^{\circ}}$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
9.【解】(1) $f(x)=\frac{1-\cos 2x}{2}+3\cdot\frac{1+\cos 2x}{2}+\sqrt{3}\sin 2x-2=\sqrt{3}\sin 2x+\cos 2x=2\sin\left(2x+\frac{\pi}{6}\right).$ 令 $-\frac{\pi}{2}+2k\pi\leqslant2x+\frac{\pi}{6}\leqslant\frac{\pi}{2}+2k\pi,k\in\mathbf{Z}$ ，解得 $-\frac{\pi}{3}+k\pi\leqslant x\leqslant\frac{\pi}{6}+k\pi,k\in\mathbf{Z}$ ，
所以 $f(x)$ 的单调递增区间为 $\left[-\frac{\pi}{3}+k\pi,\frac{\pi}{6}+k\pi\right](k\in\mathbf{Z}).$ (2) 当 $x \in \left[0, \frac{\pi}{4}\right]$ 时， $2x + \frac{\pi}{6} \in \left[\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{2\pi}{3}\right]$ ，所以 $\sin\left(2x+\frac{\pi}{6}\right) \in \left[\frac{1}{2}, 1\right]$ ，
所以 $f(x)$ 的值域为[1,2].

(3) 设 $\alpha = 2\theta + \frac{\pi}{6} \in \left(\frac{\pi}{6}, \frac{7\pi}{6}\right)$ , 则 $f(\theta) = \frac{2\sqrt{13}}{13} \Leftrightarrow \sin \left(2\theta + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) = \frac{\sqrt{13}}{13} \Leftrightarrow \sin \alpha = \frac{1}{\sqrt{13}}$ , 由于 $\frac{1}{\sqrt{13}} < \frac{1}{2}$ , 故 $\alpha \in \left(\frac{5\pi}{6}, \pi\right)$ , 所以 $\cos \alpha = -\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{\sqrt{13}}$ , 所以 $\sin 2\alpha = -\frac{4\sqrt{3}}{13}$ , $\cos 2\alpha = \frac{11}{13}$ , 故 $f(2\theta) = 2\sin \left(4\theta + \frac{\pi}{6}\right) = 2\sin \left(2\alpha - \frac{\pi}{6}\right)$ $= 2\left(\sin 2\alpha \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - \cos 2\alpha \cdot \frac{1}{2}\right)$ $= 2 \times \left(-\frac{4\sqrt{3}}{13} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} - \frac{11}{13} \times \frac{1}{2}\right) = -\frac{23}{13}$ .
