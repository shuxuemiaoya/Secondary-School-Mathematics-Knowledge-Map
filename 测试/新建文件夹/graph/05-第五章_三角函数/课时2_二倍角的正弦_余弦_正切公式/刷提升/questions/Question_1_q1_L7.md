1.[重庆西南大学附中2024高一期末] $\frac{\cos20^{\circ}-2\sin10^{\circ}}{2\sin20^{\circ}}=$ ( )
A. $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ B. $\sqrt{2}$ C. $\sqrt{3}$ D.2
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
1. C 【解析】∵ $\sin\alpha=2\sin\frac{\alpha}{2}\cos\frac{\alpha}{2}=2\times\frac{4}{5}\times\left(-\frac{3}{5}\right)=-\frac{24}{25}<0,\cos\alpha=\cos^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}-\sin^{2}\frac{\alpha}{2}=\left(-\frac{3}{5}\right)^{2}-\left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^{2}=-\frac{7}{25}<0,\therefore$ 角 $\alpha$ 是第三象限角. 故选 C.
