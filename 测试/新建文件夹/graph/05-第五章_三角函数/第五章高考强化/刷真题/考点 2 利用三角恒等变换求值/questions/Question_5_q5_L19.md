# Question 5

<!-- question-source:start -->
5.[全国新课标 I 2023·8,5 分]已知 $\sin(\alpha-\beta)=\frac{1}{3}$ , $\cos\alpha\sin\beta=\frac{1}{6}$ , 则 $\cos(2\alpha+2\beta)=$ ( )
A. $\frac{7}{9}$ B. $\frac{1}{9}$ C. $-\frac{1}{9}$ D. $-\frac{7}{9}$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
5. B

思路导引 $\sin (\alpha -\beta) = \frac{1}{3},\cos \alpha \sin \beta =$ $\frac{1}{6}\rightarrow \sin \alpha \cos \beta$ 的值 $\rightarrow \sin (\alpha +\beta)$ 的值二倍角公式 $\cos (2\alpha +2\beta)$ 的值 【解析】： $\sin (\alpha -\beta) = \sin \alpha \cos \beta -\cos \alpha \sin \beta =$ $\frac{1}{3},\cos \alpha \sin \beta = \frac{1}{6},\therefore \sin \alpha \cos \beta = \frac{1}{3} +$ cos $\alpha \sin \beta = \frac{1}{3} +\frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{2}.$ ∴ sin（α+β）= sin αcos β+cos αsin β= $\frac{1}{2}+$ $\frac{1}{6} = \frac{2}{3},\therefore \cos (2\alpha +2\beta) = \cos [2(\alpha +\beta)] = 1-$ $2\sin^2 (\alpha +\beta) = 1 - 2\times \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{9}$ ，故选B.
