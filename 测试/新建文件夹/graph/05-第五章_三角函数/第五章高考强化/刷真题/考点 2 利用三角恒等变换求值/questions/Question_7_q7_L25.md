# Question 7

<!-- question-source:start -->
7.[全国新高考I2021·6,5分]若 $\tan \theta = -2$ ，则 $\frac{\sin\theta(1 + \sin 2\theta)}{\sin\theta + \cos\theta} =$ （）A. $-\frac{6}{5}$ B. $-\frac{2}{5}$ C. $\frac{2}{5}$ D. $\frac{6}{5}$

√ 答案 P264
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
7. C 【解析】因为 $\frac{\sin\theta(1+\sin2\theta)}{\sin\theta+\cos\theta}=$ $\frac{\sin\theta(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^{2}}{\sin\theta+\cos\theta}=\sin\theta(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)=$ $\frac{\sin^{2}\theta+\sin\theta\cos\theta}{\sin^{2}\theta+\cos^{2}\theta}=\frac{\frac{\sin^{2}\theta}{\cos^{2}\theta}+\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}}{\frac{\sin^{2}\theta}{\cos^{2}\theta}+1}=$ 巧思：也可利用 $\sin\theta=-2\cos\theta$ 化为正弦或余弦求解 $\frac{\tan^{2}\theta+\tan\theta}{\tan^{2}\theta+1}=\frac{4-2}{4+1}=\frac{2}{5}$ ，故选 C.
