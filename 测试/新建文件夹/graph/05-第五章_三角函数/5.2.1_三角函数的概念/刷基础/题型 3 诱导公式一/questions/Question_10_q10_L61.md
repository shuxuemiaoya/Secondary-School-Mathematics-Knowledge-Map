10. $\sin 810^{\circ} + \tan 765^{\circ} + \tan 1125^{\circ} + \cos 360^{\circ} =$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
10.4 【解析】原式 $= \sin (2 \times 360^{\circ} + 90^{\circ}) + \tan (2 \times 360^{\circ} + 45^{\circ}) + \tan (3 \times 360^{\circ} + 45^{\circ}) + \cos (0^{\circ} + 360^{\circ}) = \sin 90^{\circ} + \tan 45^{\circ} + \tan 45^{\circ} + \cos 0^{\circ} = 4.$
