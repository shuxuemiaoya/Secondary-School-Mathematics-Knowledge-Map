# Question 3

<!-- question-source:start -->
3. (多选) [甘肃部分学校 2025 高一期末联考] 已知 $\theta \in (0, \pi)$ , $\sin \theta + \cos \theta = \frac{1}{5}$ , 则下列结论正确的是
( )
A. $\sin \theta \cos \theta = -\frac{12}{25}$ B. $\theta \in \left(\frac{\pi}{2}, \pi\right)$ C. $\sin \theta - \cos \theta = -\frac{7}{5}$ D. $\tan \theta = -\frac{4}{3}$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
3. ABD 【解析】对于A,由 $\sin\theta+\cos\theta=\frac{1}{5}$ ①,
以及 $\sin^{2}\theta+\cos^{2}\theta=1$ ,
对等式①两边取平方得 $1+2\sin\theta\cos\theta=\frac{1}{25}$ ，则 $\sin\theta\cos\theta=-\frac{12}{25}$ ②，故A正确；
对于 B, $\because\theta\in(0,\pi),\therefore\sin\theta>0$ ,结合②知 $\cos\theta<0,\therefore\theta\in\left(\frac{\pi}{2},\pi\right)$ ,故B正确;
对于 C,由上述分析知 $\sin\theta-\cos\theta=$

$\sqrt{1 - 2\sin\theta \cos\theta} = \sqrt{1 + \frac{24}{25}} = \frac{7}{5}$ 故C错误；
对于D，由方程 $\left\{ \begin{array}{l}\sin \theta -\cos \theta = \frac{7}{5},\\ \sin \theta +\cos \theta = \frac{1}{5}, \end{array} \right.$ 解得 $\left\{ \begin{array}{ll}\sin \theta = \frac{4}{5},\\ \cos \theta = -\frac{3}{5}, \end{array} \right.$ 所以 $\tan \theta = -\frac{4}{3}$ 故D正确.故选ABD.
