# Question 14

<!-- question-source:start -->
14.[广东佛山二中2025高一月考]已知 $\theta$ 是第二象限角, $\sin \theta, \cos \theta$ 是关于 $x$ 的方程 $3x^{2} - x + t = 0 (t \in \mathbf{R})$ 的两根, 则 $\cos \theta - \sin \theta =$ \_\_\_\_.
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
14. $-\frac{\sqrt{17}}{3}$ 【解析】 $\because \sin \theta, \cos \theta$ 是关于 $x$ 的方程 $3x^{2} - x + t = 0 (t \in \mathbf{R})$ 的两根， $\therefore \sin \theta + \cos \theta = \frac{1}{3}.$ $\because (\sin \theta + \cos \theta)^{2} + (\sin \theta - \cos \theta)^{2} = 2,$ $\therefore (\cos \theta - \sin \theta)^{2} = 2 - \frac{1}{9} = \frac{17}{9},$ $\therefore \cos \theta - \sin \theta = \pm \frac{\sqrt{17}}{3}.$ $\because \theta$ 是第二象限角， $\therefore \sin \theta > 0, \cos \theta < 0,$ $\therefore \cos \theta - \sin \theta < 0, \therefore \cos \theta - \sin \theta = -\frac{\sqrt{17}}{3}.$
