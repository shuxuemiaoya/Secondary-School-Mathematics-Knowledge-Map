# Question 13

<!-- question-source:start -->
13.[陕西西安高新二中2025高一月考]若 $\theta \in (0,\pi),\tan \theta +\frac{1}{\tan\theta} = 6$ ，则 $\sin \theta +\cos \theta =$ （ ）A. $\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$ B. $-\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$ C. $\pm \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$ D. $\frac{2}{3}$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
13. A 【解析】因为 $\tan\theta+\frac{1}{\tan\theta}=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}+\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}=\frac{\sin^{2}\theta+\cos^{2}\theta}{\sin\theta\cos\theta}=\frac{1}{\sin\theta\cos\theta}=6$ ，所以 $\sin\theta\cos\theta=\frac{1}{6}$ ，
所以 $(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^{2}=1+2\sin\theta\cos\theta=\frac{4}{3}.$ 又 $\theta\in(0,\pi)$ , $\sin\theta\cos\theta=\frac{1}{6}>0$ , $\sin\theta>0$ ,
则 $\cos\theta>0$ ,
所以 $\sin\theta+\cos\theta=\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{3}$ . 故选 A.
