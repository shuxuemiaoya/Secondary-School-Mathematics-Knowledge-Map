# Question 7

<!-- question-source:start -->
7. 已知 $f(\tan x)=\frac{1}{\cos^{2}x}$ ，则 $f(-\sqrt{3})=$ \_\_\_\_.
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
7.4 【解析】 $f(\tan x)=\frac{1}{\cos^{2}x}=\frac{\sin^{2}x+\cos^{2}x}{\cos^{2}x}=$ $\tan^{2}x + 1$ ，当 $\tan x = -\sqrt{3}$ 时， $f(-\sqrt{3}) = 3+$ $1 = 4.$ 巧思： $\tan x = -\sqrt{3}$ ，则 $\cos x = \pm \frac{1}{2},$ $f(-\sqrt{3}) = \frac{1}{\left(\pm\frac{1}{2}\right)^2} = 4$
<!-- answer-source:end -->
