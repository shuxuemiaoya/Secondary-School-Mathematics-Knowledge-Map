# Question 21

<!-- question-source:start -->
21.[湖南部分名校2025高一联考](1)A为△ABC的内角,已知 $\cos A=\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$ ,求 $\sin A$ , $\tan A$ 的值;
(2)已知 $\cos \alpha = \frac{1}{3} \sin \alpha$ , 求 $\sin^{2} \alpha + 2 \sin \alpha \cos \alpha$ 的值.
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
21.【解】(1)∵A为△ABC的内角，∴ $A\in(0,\pi)$ .
又 $\cos A>0,\therefore A\in\left(0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right),\therefore\sin A>0,$ 由 $\sin^{2}A=1-\cos^{2}A=\frac{1}{9}$ ，得 $\sin A=\frac{1}{3}$ , $\therefore \tan A = \frac{\sin A}{\cos A} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}.$ (2)由 $\cos\alpha=\frac{1}{3}\sin\alpha$ ，得 $\tan\alpha=3$ ，
又 $\sin^{2}\alpha+\cos^{2}\alpha=1$ ,
∴ 原式 $=\frac{\sin^{2}\alpha+2\sin\alpha\cos\alpha}{\sin^{2}\alpha+\cos^{2}\alpha}$ $=\frac{\tan^{2}\alpha+2\tan\alpha}{\tan^{2}\alpha+1}=\frac{3}{2}.$
<!-- answer-source:end -->
