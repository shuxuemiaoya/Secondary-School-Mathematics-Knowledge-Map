# Question 10

<!-- question-source:start -->
10. 已知 $\alpha$ 为第二象限角, 则 $\frac{2\sin \alpha}{\sqrt{1 - \cos^2 \alpha}} + \cos \alpha \cdot \sqrt{1 + \tan^2 \alpha}$ 的值是 \_\_\_\_.
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
10.1 【解析】∵ $\alpha$ 为第二象限角, ∴ $\sin \alpha > 0$ , $\cos \alpha < 0$ ,

∴ $\frac{2\sin \alpha}{\sqrt{1 - \cos^{2} \alpha}} + \cos \alpha \sqrt{1 + \tan^{2} \alpha} = \frac{2\sin \alpha}{\sin \alpha} +$ → 点悟: $\sqrt{1 + \tan^{2} \alpha} =$ $\sqrt{1 + \frac{\sin^{2} \alpha}{\cos^{2} \alpha}} = \sqrt{\frac{\cos^{2} \alpha + \sin^{2} \alpha}{\cos^{2} \alpha}} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{\cos^{2} \alpha}}$ $\cos \alpha \sqrt{\frac{1}{\cos^{2} \alpha}} = 2 + \cos \alpha \left| \frac{1}{\cos \alpha} \right| = 2 - 1 = 1.$
