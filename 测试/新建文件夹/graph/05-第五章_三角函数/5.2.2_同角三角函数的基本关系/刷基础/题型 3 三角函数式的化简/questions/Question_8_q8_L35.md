# Question 8

<!-- question-source:start -->
8. 已知 $\alpha$ 为第二象限角, 化简 $\tan \alpha \cdot \sqrt{\frac{1}{\sin^2 \alpha} - 1} =$ ( )
A. 1 B. 2 C. -1 D. $\frac{1}{2}$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
8. C 【解析】∵ $\alpha$ 为第二象限角，
∴ $\tan \alpha \cdot \sqrt{\frac{1}{\sin^{2}\alpha} - 1} = \tan \alpha \cdot \sqrt{\frac{1 - \sin^{2}\alpha}{\sin^{2}\alpha}} = \tan \alpha \times \frac{-\cos \alpha}{\sin \alpha} = -1$ ，故选 C.
点悟：分子 $\sqrt{1-\sin^{2}\alpha}=\sqrt{\cos^{2}\alpha}$
