# Question 1

<!-- question-source:start -->
1.[福建泉州四校2025高一期中联考]命题p:无论x取何实数,必有 $|x|\geqslant0$ ,则 $\neg p$ 为( )
A. $\forall x\in R$ ,都有 $|x|\geqslant0$ B. $\forall x\in R$ ,都有 $|x|<0$ C. $\exists x_{0}\in R$ ,使得 $|x_{0}|\geqslant0$ D. $\exists x_{0}\in R$ ,使得 $|x_{0}|<0$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
1. D 【解析】由题意可知,命题 $p: \forall x \in R$ , $|x| \geqslant 0$ , 该命题为全称量词命题, 故 $\neg p: \exists x_{0} \in R$ , 使得 $|x_{0}| < 0$ , 故选 D.
<!-- answer-source:end -->
