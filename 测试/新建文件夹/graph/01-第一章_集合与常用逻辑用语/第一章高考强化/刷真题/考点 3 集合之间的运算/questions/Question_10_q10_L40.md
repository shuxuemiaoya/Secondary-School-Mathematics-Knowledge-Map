# Question 10

<!-- question-source:start -->
10.[全国乙文2023·2,5分]设全集 $U = \{0,1,2,$ 4,6,8},集合 $M = \{0,4,6\} ,N = \{0,1,6\}$ ，则 $M\cup \complement_U N =$ （ ）A. $\{0,2,4,6,8\}$ B. $\{0,1,4,6,8\}$ C. $\{1,2,4,6,8\}$ D. $U$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
10. A 【解析】因为全集 $U = \{0, 1, 2, 4, 6, 8\}$ ，集合 $N = \{0, 1, 6\}$ ，所以 $\complement_{U} N = \{2, 4, 8\}$ 。因为 $M = \{0, 4, 6\}$ ，所以 $M \cup \complement_{U} N = \{0, 2, 4, 6, 8\}$ 。故选 A.
