# Question 12

<!-- question-source:start -->
12.[全国甲理 2022·3,5 分]设全集 $U = \{-2, -1,0,1,2,3\}$ , 集合 $A = \{-1,2\}, B = \{x \mid x^2 - 4x + 3 = 0\}$ , 则 $\complement_U(A \cup B) =$ ( )
A. $\{1,3\}$ B. $\{0,3\}$ C. $\{-2,1\}$ D. $\{-2,0\}$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
12. D 【解析】因为 $x^{2}-4x+3=0$ ，解得 $x_{1}=1, x_{2}=3$ ，所以 $B=\{1,3\}$ ，
所以 $A \cup B = \{-1, 1, 2, 3\}$ ,
所以 $\complement_{U}(A \cup B) = \{-2, 0\}$ . 故选 D.
