# Question 3

<!-- question-source:start -->
3.[全国甲理2024·15,5分]已知a>1且 $\frac{1}{\log_{8}a}-\frac{1}{\log_{a}4}=-\frac{5}{2}$ ，则a= \_\_\_\_.
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
3.64 【解析】因为 $\frac{1}{\log_8a} - \frac{1}{\log_a4} = \frac{3}{\log_2a} - \frac{1}{2}\log_2a = -\frac{5}{2}$ , 所以 $(\log_2a + 1)(\log_2a - 6) = 0$ . 又 $a > 1$ , 故 $\log_2a = 6$ , 解得 $a = 64$ .
<!-- answer-source:end -->
