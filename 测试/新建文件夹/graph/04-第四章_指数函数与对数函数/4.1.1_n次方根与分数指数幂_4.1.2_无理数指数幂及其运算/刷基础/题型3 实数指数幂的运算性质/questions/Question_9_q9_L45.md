# Question 9

<!-- question-source:start -->
9.[吉林长春五中2025高一月考]已知正数a,b满足 $\sqrt[4]{4}\times\sqrt[6]{8}=2$ ,则3a+2b的最小值为( )
A. 10    B. 12    C. 18    D. 24
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
9. D 【解析】∵ $\sqrt[4]{4} \times \sqrt[6]{8} = 2^{\frac{2}{a}} \times 2^{\frac{3}{b}} = 2^{\frac{2}{a} + \frac{3}{b}} = 2$ ,
∴ $\frac{2}{a}+\frac{3}{b}=1$ ,
∴ $3a+2b=\left(\frac{2}{a}+\frac{3}{b}\right)(3a+2b)=6+6+\frac{4b}{a}+$ $\frac{9a}{b}\geqslant12+2\sqrt{\frac{4b}{a}\cdot\frac{9a}{b}}=12+2\times6=24,$ 当且仅当 $\left\{\begin{aligned}\frac{4b}{a}&=\frac{9a}{b},\\ \frac{2}{a}&+\frac{3}{b}=1,\end{aligned}\right.$ 即 $\left\{\begin{aligned}a&=4,\\ b&=6\end{aligned}\right.$ 时，等号成立.故选D.
