# Question 6

6.[天津第一中学2025高一期末]计算 $(\log_43 + \log_83)\times (\log_32 + \log_92) - e^{\ln 2} =$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
6. $-\frac{3}{4}$ 【解析】 $(\log_43 + \log_83)\times (\log_32+$ $\log_92) - \mathrm{e}^{\ln 2}$ $= (\log_{2^2}3 + \log_{2^3}3)\times (\log_32 + \log_3^22) - \mathrm{e}^{\ln 2}$ $= \left(\frac{1}{2}\log_23 + \frac{1}{3}\log_23\right)\times \left(\log_32 + \frac{1}{2}\log_32\right) - 2$ $= \frac{5}{6}\times \log_23\times \frac{3}{2}\times \log_32 - 2 = \frac{5}{4} -2 = -\frac{3}{4}.$
