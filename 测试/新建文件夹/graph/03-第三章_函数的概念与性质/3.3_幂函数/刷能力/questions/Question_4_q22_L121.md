# Question 4

<!-- question-source:start -->
4.[湖北部分学校2025高一联考]设 $a=\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$ ,b= $\left(\frac{4}{3}\right)^{\frac{1}{4}},c=\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{\frac{3}{4}}$ ，则a,b,c的大小关系是（）
A. c<a<b
B. c<b<a
C. a<c<b
D. b<c<a
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
4. A 【解析】因为 $a=\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}}$ , $c=\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{\frac{3}{4}}$ ,
所以 $a = \left[\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{2}\right]^{\frac{1}{4}} = \left(\frac{9}{16}\right)^{\frac{1}{4}}$ , c = $\left[\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^{3}\right]^{\frac{1}{4}}=\left(\frac{8}{27}\right)^{\frac{1}{4}}.$ 因为函数 $y=x^{\frac{1}{4}}$ 为增函数， $\frac{8}{27}<\frac{9}{16}<\frac{4}{3}$

所以 $\left(\frac{8}{27}\right)^{\frac{1}{4}}<\left(\frac{9}{16}\right)^{\frac{1}{4}}<\left(\frac{4}{3}\right)^{\frac{1}{4}}$ ，故c<a<b.
故选 A.
