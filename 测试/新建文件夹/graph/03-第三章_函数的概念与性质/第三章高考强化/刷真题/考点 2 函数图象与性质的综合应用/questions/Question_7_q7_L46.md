# Question 7

<!-- question-source:start -->
7.[全国甲文2021·12,5分]设 $f(x)$ 是定义域为R的奇函数,且 $f(1+x)=f(-x)$ .若 $f\left(-\frac{1}{3}\right)=\frac{1}{3}$ ,则 $f\left(\frac{5}{3}\right)=$ ( )
A. $-\frac{5}{3}$ B. $-\frac{1}{3}$ C. $\frac{1}{3}$ D. $\frac{5}{3}$
<!-- question-source:end -->

## 答案与解析

<!-- answer-source:start -->
7.C 【解析】由题知 $f(2+x)=f(1+(1+x))=f(-1-x)=-f(1+x)=-f(-x)=f(x)$ ，则函数 $f(x)$ 是周期为2的周期函数，则 $f\left(\frac{5}{3}\right)=f\left(-\frac{1}{3}\right)=\frac{1}{3}$ ，故选C.
快解 $f\left(\frac{5}{3}\right)=f\left(1+\frac{2}{3}\right)=f\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)=-f\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)=-f\left(1-\frac{1}{3}\right)=-f\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)=f\left(-\frac{1}{3}\right)=\frac{1}{3}.$
