设 $\boldsymbol{a} = (a_1, a_2, a_3)$，$\boldsymbol{b} = (b_1, b_2, b_3)$，则：
$$\boldsymbol{a} + \boldsymbol{b} = (a_1 + b_1, a_2 + b_2, a_3 + b_3)$$
$$\boldsymbol{a} - \boldsymbol{b} = (a_1 - b_1, a_2 - b_2, a_3 - b_3)$$
$$\lambda \boldsymbol{a} = (\lambda a_1, \lambda a_2, \lambda a_3), \quad \lambda \in \mathbf{R}$$
$$\boldsymbol{a} \cdot \boldsymbol{b} = a_1 b_1 + a_2 b_2 + a_3 b_3$$
