##### 题型05 平面向量投影（投影向量）
##### 【典例1】（2023·全国·模拟预测）已知向量$\overrightarrow{a}$，$\overrightarrow{b}$满足$\overrightarrow{a} = \left( 1 , 2 \sqrt{2} \right)$，$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \left( \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} \right) = 0$，则$\overrightarrow{b}$在$\overrightarrow{a}$方向上的投影向量的模为（ ）
$\quad$A．$\frac{3 \sqrt{3}}{2}$ 
$\quad$B．3 
$\quad$C．$3 \sqrt{3}$ 
$\quad$D．
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>B
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$\overrightarrow{a} = \left( 1 , 2 \sqrt{2} \right)$，所以$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| = 3$，又$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \left( \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} \right) = \overrightarrow{a}^{2} + \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 0$，
所以$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = - 9$，则$\overrightarrow{b}$在$\overrightarrow{a}$方向上的投影向量的模为$\left| \overrightarrow{b} \text{cos} \overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b} \right| = \frac{\left| \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} \right|}{\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|} = \frac{9}{3} = 3$，
故选：B．
##### 【典例2】（2023·广东·统考二模）已知$O$是坐标原点，点$N \left( \sqrt{2} , 1 \right)$，且点$M$是圆$C$：$x^{2} + y^{2} - 2 x - 2 y + 1 = 0$上的一点，则向量$\overrightarrow{O N}$在向量$\overrightarrow{O M}$上的投影向量的模的取值范围是 .
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$\left\lbrack 1 , \sqrt{3} \right\rbrack$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>设直线$O M$倾斜角为$\alpha$，$O N$的倾斜角为$\beta$，
当直线$O M$的斜率存在时，设直线$O M$方程为$y = k x$，即$k x - y = 0$
由圆$C$：$x^{2} + y^{2} - 2 x - 2 y + 1 = 0$，即$( x - 1 )^{2} + ( y - 1 )^{2} = 1$，
所以圆心$C ( 1 , 1 )$，半径$r = 1$，
又点$M$在圆上，
所以点$C$到直线$O M$的距离$d = \frac{| k - 1 |}{\sqrt{k^{2} + 1}} \leq r$，解得$k \geq 0$，即$\alpha \in \left. 0 , \frac{\pi}{2} \right)$，
当直线$O M$的斜率不存在时，$O M$方程为$x = 0$与圆$C$相切，成立，此时$\alpha = \frac{\pi}{2}$，
综上$\alpha \in \left\lbrack 0 , \frac{\pi}{2} \right\rbrack$，$\tan \alpha \in \left. 0 , + \infty )$，
则$\tan ( \alpha - \beta ) = \frac{\tan \alpha - \tan \beta}{1 + \tan \alpha \cdot \tan \beta} = \frac{\tan \alpha - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}}{1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \tan \alpha} = \sqrt{2} - \frac{3 \sqrt{2}}{2 + \sqrt{2} \tan \alpha}$，
所以$\tan ( \alpha - \beta ) \in \left. - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} , \sqrt{2} \right.$，即$\tan | \alpha - \beta | \in \left\lbrack 0 , \sqrt{2} \right\rbrack$
所以$\cos | \alpha - \beta | \in \left\lbrack \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} , 1 \right\rbrack$，
即$\cos \left\langle \overrightarrow{O M} , \overrightarrow{O N} \right\rangle = \cos | \alpha - \beta | \in \left\lbrack \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3} , 1 \right\rbrack$，
又$\left| \overrightarrow{O N} \right| = \sqrt{\left( \sqrt{2} \right)^{2} + 1^{2}} = \sqrt{3}$
所以向量$\overrightarrow{O N}$在向量$\overrightarrow{O M}$上的投影向量的模为$\left| \left| \overrightarrow{O N} \right| \cos \left\langle \overrightarrow{O M} , \overrightarrow{O N} \right\rangle \right| \in \left\lbrack 1 , \sqrt{3} \right\rbrack$，
故答案为：$\left\lbrack 1 , \sqrt{3} \right\rbrack$.
##### 【典例3】（2022上·重庆沙坪坝·高三重庆八中校考阶段练习）已知对任意平面向量$\overrightarrow{A B} = ( x , y )$，把*B*绕其起点沿逆时针方向旋转$\theta$得到向量$\overrightarrow{A P} = \left( x \cos \theta - y \sin \theta , x \cos \theta + y \sin \theta \right)$叫做把点*B*绕点*A*沿逆时针方向旋转$\theta$得到点*P*.已知平面内点$A ( 2 , 1 )$，点$B \left( 2 + \sqrt{2} , 1 - \sqrt{2} \right)$，把点*B*绕点*A*沿逆时针$\frac{\pi}{4}$后得到点*P*，向量$\overrightarrow{a}$为向量$\overrightarrow{P B}$在向量$\overrightarrow{P A}$上的投影向量，则$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| =$ .
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$2 - \sqrt{2}$/$- \sqrt{2} + 2$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$A ( 2 , 1 )$，$B \left( 2 + \sqrt{2} , 1 - \sqrt{2} \right)$，所以$\overrightarrow{A B} = ( \sqrt{2} , - \sqrt{2} )$，
$\overrightarrow{A P} = ( \sqrt{2} \cos \frac{\pi}{4} - ( - \sqrt{2} ) \sin \frac{\pi}{4} , \sqrt{2} \cos \frac{\pi}{4} + ( - \sqrt{2} ) \sin \frac{\pi}{4} ) = ( 2 , 0 )$，
所以*P*点坐标为$( 4 , 1 )$，
所以$\overrightarrow{P B} = \left( \sqrt{2} - 2 , - \sqrt{2} \right)$，$\overrightarrow{P A} = ( - 2 , 0 )$
所以$\left| \frac{\overrightarrow{P B} \cdot \overrightarrow{P A}}{\left| \overrightarrow{P A} \right|} \cdot \frac{\overrightarrow{P A}}{\left| \overrightarrow{P A} \right|} \right| = \left| \frac{4 - 2 \sqrt{2}}{2} \cdot \frac{( - 2 , 0 )}{2} \right| = 2 - \sqrt{2}$.
故答案为：$2 - \sqrt{2}$.
##### 【变式1】（2023上·重庆·高三校联考阶段练习）设向量$\overrightarrow{A B} = \left( \frac{1}{x^{2}} , x^{2} \right)$在向量$\overrightarrow{A C} = ( 2 , 2 )$上的投影向量为，则$\lambda$的最小值为（ ）
$\quad$A．$\frac{1}{2}$ 
$\quad$B．$\frac{2 \sqrt{10}}{5}$ 
$\quad$C．1 
$\quad$D．$\frac{4}{5}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>A
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>依题意，$x \neq 0 , x^{2} > 0$，
向量$\overrightarrow{A B} = \left( \frac{1}{x^{2}} , x^{2} \right)$在向量$\overrightarrow{A C} = ( 2 , 2 )$上的投影向量：
$\frac{\overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A C}}{\left| \overrightarrow{A C} \right|} \cdot \frac{\overrightarrow{A C}}{\left| \overrightarrow{A C} \right|} = \frac{\overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A C}}{\left| \overrightarrow{A C} \right|^{2}} \cdot \overrightarrow{A C} = \lambda \overrightarrow{A C}$，
所以$\lambda = \frac{\overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A C}}{\left| \overrightarrow{A C} \right|^{2}} = \frac{\frac{2}{x^{2}} + 2 x^{2}}{8} = \frac{\frac{1}{x^{2}} + x^{2}}{4} \geq \frac{2 \sqrt{\frac{1}{x^{2}} \cdot x^{2}}}{4} = \frac{1}{2}$，
当且仅当$\frac{1}{x^{2}} = x^{2} , x = \pm 1$时等号成立.
故选：A

##### 【变式3】（2023下·广东惠州·高一校联考阶段练习）已知$\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| = 2 \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|$，若$\overrightarrow{a}$与$\overrightarrow{b}$的夹角为$12 0^{\circ}$，则在$\overrightarrow{b}$上的投影向量为 .
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$- \frac{3}{2} \overrightarrow{b}$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| = 2 \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|$，$\overrightarrow{a}$与$\overrightarrow{b}$的夹角$12 0^{\circ}$，则$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| \cos 1 2 0^{\circ} = 2 \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|^{2} \times \left( - \frac{1}{2} \right) = - \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|^{2}$，
所以$( 2 \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} ) \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 2 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} - \overrightarrow{b}^{2} = - 2 \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|^{2} - 4 \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|^{2} = - 6 \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|^{2}$，
所以在$\overrightarrow{b}$上的投影向量为$\frac{( 2 \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} ) \cdot \overrightarrow{b}}{\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|} \cdot \frac{\overrightarrow{b}}{\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|} = \frac{- 6 \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|^{2}}{4 \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|^{2}} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = - \frac{3}{2} \overrightarrow{b}$.
故答案为：$- \frac{3}{2} \overrightarrow{b}$
