##### 题型02由终边或终边上点求三角函数值
##### 【典例1】（23-24高一上·江西鹰潭·期末）若角$\alpha$的终边经过点$P ( 3 m , - 4 m )$（$m < 0$），则$\text{sin} \alpha + \text{cos} \alpha =$（ ）
$\quad$A．$\frac{7}{5}$ 
$\quad$B．$- \frac{7}{5}$ 
$\quad$C．$\frac{1}{5}$ 
$\quad$D．$- \frac{1}{5}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>C
<span class="fake-tag">分析</span>应用任意角三角函数定义求正弦值和余弦值再计算即可.
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>$r = | O P | = \sqrt{( 3 m )^{2} + ( - 4 m )^{2}} = 5 | m | = - 5 m$，$O$为坐标原点，
则$\text{sin} \alpha = \frac{y}{r} = \frac{- 4 m}{- 5 m} = \frac{4}{5}$，$\text{cos} \alpha = \frac{x}{r} = \frac{3 m}{- 5 m} = - \frac{3}{5}$，
故$\text{sin} \alpha + \text{cos} \alpha = \frac{4}{5} - \frac{3}{5} = \frac{1}{5}$.
故选：C.
##### 【典例2】（23-24高一下·辽宁·阶段练习）若角$\alpha$的终边经过点$( 1 , - 2 )$，则$\frac{\text{sin} \alpha + 3 \text{co} \text{s}^{3} \alpha}{6 \text{co} \text{s}^{3} \alpha - 2 \text{sin} \alpha \text{co} \text{s}^{2} \alpha} + \text{si} \text{n}^{2} \alpha =$（ ）
$\quad$A．$- \frac{\sqrt{5}}{10}$ 
$\quad$B． 
$\quad$C．$\frac{1}{2}$ 
$\quad$D．$\frac{1}{10}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>D
<span class="fake-tag">分析</span>根据三角函数的定义求出$\sin \alpha$，$\cos \alpha$，再代入计算可得.
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为角$\alpha$的终边经过点$( 1 , - 2 )$，
所以$\sin \alpha = \frac{- 2}{\sqrt{1^{2} + ( - 2 )^{2}}} = - \frac{2 \sqrt{5}}{5}$，$\cos \alpha = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1^{2} + ( - 2 )^{2}}} = \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5}$，
所以$\frac{\text{sin} \alpha + 3 \text{co} \text{s}^{3} \alpha}{6 \text{co} \text{s}^{3} \alpha - 2 \text{sin} \alpha \text{co} \text{s}^{2} \alpha} + \text{si} \text{n}^{2} \alpha$
$= \frac{- \frac{2 \sqrt{5}}{5} + 3 \left( \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5} \right)^{3}}{6 \left( \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5} \right)^{3} - 2 \left( - \frac{2 \sqrt{5}}{5} \right) \times \left( \frac{\sqrt{5}}{5} \right)^{2}} + \left( - \frac{2 \sqrt{5}}{5} \right)^{2} = \frac{1}{10}$.
故选：D
