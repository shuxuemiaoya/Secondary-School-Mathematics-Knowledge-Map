##### 题型06 三点共线问题
##### 【典例1】（2023下·贵州遵义·高一校考阶段练习）已知不共线的向量$\overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b}$，且$\overrightarrow{A B} = \overrightarrow{a} + 2 \overrightarrow{b}$，$\overrightarrow{B C} = - 5 \overrightarrow{a} + 6 \overrightarrow{b}$，$\overrightarrow{C D} = 7 \overrightarrow{a} - 2 \overrightarrow{b}$，则一定共线的三点是（ ）
$\quad$A．*A*，*B*，*D* 
$\quad$B．*A*，*B*，*C*
$\quad$C．*B*，*C*，*D* 
$\quad$D．*A*，*C*，*D*
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>A
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>对A，,
所以$\overrightarrow{A D} = 3 \overrightarrow{A B}$，则$A , B , D$三点共线，A正确；
对B，$\overrightarrow{A C} = \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{B C} = - 4 \overrightarrow{a} + 8 \overrightarrow{b}$,
则不存在任何$\lambda \in R$，使得$\overrightarrow{A C} = \lambda \overrightarrow{A B}$，所以不共线,B错误；
对C，$\overrightarrow{B D} = \overrightarrow{B C} + \overrightarrow{C D} = 2 \overrightarrow{a} + 4 \overrightarrow{b}$,
则不存在任何$\mu \in R$，使得$\overrightarrow{B D} = \mu \overrightarrow{B C}$，所以$B , C , D$不共线，C错误；
对D，$\overrightarrow{A C} = \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{B C} = - 4 \overrightarrow{a} + 8 \overrightarrow{b}$,
则不存在任何$t \in R$，使得$\overrightarrow{C D} = t \overrightarrow{A C}$，所以$A , C , D$不共线，D错误；
故选:A.
##### 【典例2】（2023下·安徽合肥·高一统考期中）设$\overrightarrow{e}_{1} , \overrightarrow{e}_{2}$是不共线的两个向量，$\overrightarrow{A B} = \overrightarrow{e}_{1} + k \overrightarrow{e}_{2} , \overrightarrow{C B} = \overrightarrow{e}_{1} + 3 \overrightarrow{e}_{2} , \overrightarrow{C D} = 2 \overrightarrow{e}_{1} - \overrightarrow{e}_{2}$.若$A , B , D$三点共线，则*k*的值为 .
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$- 4$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$A , B , D$三点共线，故$\overrightarrow{A B}\overrightarrow{B D}$,
则$\exists \lambda \in R$，使得$\overrightarrow{A B} = \lambda \overrightarrow{B D}$，
又$\overrightarrow{B D} = \overrightarrow{C D} - \overrightarrow{C B} = 2 \overrightarrow{e}_{1} - \overrightarrow{e}_{2} - ( \overrightarrow{e}_{1} + 3 \overrightarrow{e}_{2} ) = \overrightarrow{e}_{1} - 4 \overrightarrow{e}_{2}$，
故$\overrightarrow{e}_{1} + k \overrightarrow{e}_{2} = \lambda ( \overrightarrow{e}_{1} - 4 \overrightarrow{e}_{2} )$，则$\begin{cases}\lambda = 1 \\ - 4 \lambda = k\end{cases}$，解得$k = - 4$，
故答案为：$- 4$
##### 【典例3】（2023上·江西·高二校联考开学考试）已知$\overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b}$是平面内不共线的单位向量，$O , A , B , C$是该平面内的点，且$\overrightarrow{O A} = \overrightarrow{a} - 2 \overrightarrow{b}$，$\overrightarrow{O B} = 2 \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b}$，$\overrightarrow{O C} = 3 \overrightarrow{a} + t \overrightarrow{b}$.
(1)若$\left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right| = 3$，求$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b}$；
(2)若三点共线，求实数$t$的值.
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>(1)$- \frac{1}{6}$
(2)$t = 4$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>（1）$\because \overrightarrow{A B} = \overrightarrow{O B} - \overrightarrow{O A} = \overrightarrow{a} + 3 \overrightarrow{b}$，$\therefore \left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right|^{2} = \left| \overrightarrow{a} + 3 \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} = \overrightarrow{a}^{2} + 6 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} + 9 \overrightarrow{b}^{2} = 1 0 + 6 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 9$，
$\therefore \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = - \frac{1}{6}$.
（2）$\because \overrightarrow{A B} = \overrightarrow{a} + 3 \overrightarrow{b}$，$\overrightarrow{A C} = \overrightarrow{O C} - \overrightarrow{O A} = 2 \overrightarrow{a} + ( t + 2 ) \overrightarrow{b}$，
又三点共线，即$\overrightarrow{A B} , \overrightarrow{A C}$共线，
$\therefore 1 \times ( t + 2 ) = 3 \times 2$，解得：$t = 4$.

##### 【变式3】（2023下·山东泰安·高一校考阶段练习）如图，在$\bigtriangleup A B C$中，$\overrightarrow{A M} = \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{A B} , \overrightarrow{B N} = \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{B C}$.设$\overrightarrow{A B} = \overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{A C} = \overrightarrow{b}$.
![@@@063c72a4-bf9e-4e8c-9567-840c8b88c4db](高中习题/课堂同步/随堂练习习题/media/69d1105b8c0275999ab71242e6c64915.png)
(1)用$\overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{b}$表示$\overrightarrow{B C} , \overrightarrow{M N}$；
(2)若$P$为$\bigtriangleup A B C$内部一点，且$\overrightarrow{A P} = \frac{5}{12} \overrightarrow{a} + \frac{1}{4} \overrightarrow{b}$.求证：$M , P , N$三点共线，并指明点$P$的具体位置.
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>(1)$\overrightarrow{B C} = \overrightarrow{b} - \overrightarrow{a}$，$\overrightarrow{M N} = \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{b} + \frac{1}{6} \overrightarrow{a}$
(2)证明见解析，$P$是$M N$的中点
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>（1）依题意，$\overrightarrow{B C} = \overrightarrow{A C} - \overrightarrow{A B} = \overrightarrow{b} - \overrightarrow{a}$，
$\overrightarrow{M N} = \overrightarrow{B N} - \overrightarrow{B M} = \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{B C} + \frac{2}{3} \overrightarrow{A B} = \frac{1}{2} \left( \overrightarrow{b} - \overrightarrow{a} \right) + \frac{2 \overrightarrow{a}}{3} = \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{b} + \frac{1}{6} \overrightarrow{a}$.-
（2）由$\overrightarrow{A M} + \overrightarrow{A N} = \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{A C} + \overrightarrow{C N} = \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{A C} - \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{B C} = \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} - \frac{1}{2} \left( \overrightarrow{b} - \overrightarrow{a} \right) = \frac{5}{6} \overrightarrow{a} + \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{b}$，
又$\overrightarrow{A P} = \frac{5}{12} \overrightarrow{a} + \frac{1}{4} \overrightarrow{b}$，所以$\overrightarrow{A P} = \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A M} + \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A N}$，
$\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = 1$，故$M , P , N$三点共线，且$P$是$M N$的中点.
