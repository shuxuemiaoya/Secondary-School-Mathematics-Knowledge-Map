##### 【变式2】（23-24高一·全国·课堂例题）不等式$\sin \left( 2 x + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) \geq \frac{1}{2}$的解集为 ．
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$\left\{ x | - \frac{\pi}{12} + k \pi \leq x \leq \frac{\pi}{4} + k \pi , k \in Z \right\}$
<span class="fake-tag">分析</span>可先求出$\sin \alpha \geq \frac{1}{2}$，$\alpha \in [ 0 , 2 \pi ]$的解集，在将$2 x + \frac{\pi}{3}$代替$\alpha$解出$x$，则不等式$\sin \left( 2 x + \frac{\pi}{3} \right) \geq \frac{1}{2}$的解集可求.
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>画出$x \in \lbrack 0 , 2 \pi \rbrack$时，$y = \sin x$的图象．
![@@@76669d92-5aa1-44dc-80aa-4db465115f07](MathLogic-Weave/高中数学逻辑结构/题/Attachments/887fa6cee9d436560a2282b7dc0afd1e.png)
令$\sin \alpha = \frac{1}{2}$，$\alpha \in [ 0 , 2 \pi ]$，解得$\alpha = \frac{\pi}{6}$或$\alpha = \frac{5 \pi}{6}$
又$y = \sin x$的周期为$2 \pi$，所以$\sin \alpha \geq \frac{1}{2}$的解集为$\left\{ \alpha | \frac{\pi}{6} + 2 k \pi \leq \alpha \leq \frac{\text{5} \text{π}}{6} + 2 k \pi , k \in Z \right\}$．

故答案为：$\left\{ x | - \frac{\pi}{12} + k \pi \leq x \leq \frac{\pi}{4} + k \pi , k \in Z \right\}$.
