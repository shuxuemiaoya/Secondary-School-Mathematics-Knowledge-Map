$\quad$A．$\frac{1}{3} \left( \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right)$ 
$\quad$B．$\frac{1}{2} \left( \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right)$ 
$\quad$C．$\frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{a} - \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{b}$ 
$\quad$D．$\frac{2}{3} \left( \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right)$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>D
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>由题意可知，$\overrightarrow{a} = \frac{1}{2} \left( \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{A C} \right)$，$\overrightarrow{b} = \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{C A} = \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{A B} - \overrightarrow{A C}$．
