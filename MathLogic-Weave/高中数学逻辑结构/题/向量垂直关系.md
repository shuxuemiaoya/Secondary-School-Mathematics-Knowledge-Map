所以$\left( \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} \right) \cdot \left( \overrightarrow{a} - 3 \overrightarrow{b} \right) = \overrightarrow{a}^{2} - 2 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} - 3 \overrightarrow{b}^{2} = 0$，解得$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 1$，

故答案为：$\left( \frac{2}{5} , \frac{1}{5} \right)$
