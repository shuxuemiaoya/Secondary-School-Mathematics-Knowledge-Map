##### 题型03 利用向量求线段长度
##### 【典例1】（2023下·湖南永州·高一统考期末）一个人骑自行车由*A*地出发向东骑行了$6 \text{km}$到达*B*地，由*B*地向南东$3 0 {^\circ}$方向骑行了$6 \text{km}$到达*C*地，从*C*地向北偏东$6 0 {^\circ}$骑行了$2 \sqrt{3} \text{km}$到达*D*地，则*A*，*D*两地的距离是 $\text{km}$．
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$2 \sqrt{39}$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>以*A*为原点，*AB*所在直线为*x*轴建立直角坐标系，如图，
![@@@e34cdb91-1d8e-41a3-82ed-b41ebc950c10](MathLogic-Weave/高中数学逻辑结构/题/Attachments/87205ff1c2930d491dd882599fee91bd.png)
则，$C ( 6 + 6 \sin 3 0 {^\circ} , - 6 \cos 3 0 {^\circ} )$，即$C ( 9 , - 3 \sqrt{3} )$，
$D ( 9 + 2 \sqrt{3} \sin 6 0 {^\circ} , - 3 \sqrt{3} + 2 \sqrt{3} \cos 6 0 {^\circ} )$，即$D ( 1 2 , - 2 \sqrt{3} )$，
所以$\overrightarrow{A D} = ( 1 2 , - 2 \sqrt{3} )$，故$\left| \overrightarrow{A D} \right| = \sqrt{1 2^{2} + ( - 2 \sqrt{3} )^{2}} = 2 \sqrt{39}$.
所以*A*，*D*两地距离为$2 \sqrt{39} \text{km}$.
故答案为：$2 \sqrt{39}$.
##### 【典例2】（2023下·河北沧州·高一校考阶段练习）如图，在$\bigtriangleup A B C$中，$A B = 4 , A C = 6 , \overrightarrow{B D} = \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{D C} , \overrightarrow{B E} = \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{E A} , \overrightarrow{A F} = \frac{1}{2} \overrightarrow{F C} , \overrightarrow{D E} \cdot \overrightarrow{D F} = - 4$.
![@@@12579175-ccb4-4d1a-897e-65f0f2998802](MathLogic-Weave/高中数学逻辑结构/题/Attachments/1a3831d221900d2ed4d5e7eb9cedf93d.png)
(1)求$B C$的长；
(2)求$A D$的长.
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>(1)$2 \sqrt{22}$
(2)$\frac{2 \sqrt{7}}{3}$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>（1）$\overrightarrow{D E} = \overrightarrow{D B} + \overrightarrow{B E} = \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{C B} - \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{A B} = \frac{1}{3} \left( \overrightarrow{A B} - \overrightarrow{A C} \right) - \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{A B} = - \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{A C}$；
$\overrightarrow{D F} = \overrightarrow{D C} + \overrightarrow{C F} = \frac{2}{3} \overrightarrow{B C} + \frac{2}{3} \overrightarrow{C A} = \frac{2}{3} \left( - \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{A C} \right) - \frac{2}{3} \overrightarrow{A C} = - \frac{2}{3} \overrightarrow{A B}$，
$\overrightarrow{D E} \cdot \overrightarrow{D F} = \frac{2}{9} \overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A C} = - 4$，故$\overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A C} = - 18$，
$\left| \overrightarrow{B C} \right| = \sqrt{\left( \overrightarrow{A C} - \overrightarrow{A B} \right)^{2}} = \sqrt{\overrightarrow{A C}^{2} - 2 \overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A C} + \overrightarrow{A B}^{2}} = \sqrt{1 6 + 3 6 + 3 6} = 2 \sqrt{22}$.
（2）$\overrightarrow{A D} = \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{B D} = \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{B C} = \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{3} \left( \overrightarrow{A C} - \overrightarrow{A B} \right) = \frac{2}{3} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{A C}$，
$\left| \overrightarrow{A D} \right| = \sqrt{\left( \frac{2}{3} \overrightarrow{A B} + \frac{1}{3} \overrightarrow{A C} \right)^{2}} = \sqrt{\frac{4}{9} \overrightarrow{A B}^{2} + \frac{4}{9} \overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A C} + \frac{1}{9} \overrightarrow{A C}^{2}}$
$= \sqrt{\frac{4}{9} \times 16 - \frac{4}{9} \times 1 8 + \frac{1}{9} \times 36} = \frac{2 \sqrt{7}}{3}$.
##### 【变式1】（2023下·河北石家庄·高一石家庄市第四中学校考阶段练习）已知的夹角为$\frac{\pi}{3}$，则三角形$A B C$的$B C$边上中线的长为 .
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$\frac{\sqrt{13}}{2}$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>设*D*为$B C$的中点，则$2 \overrightarrow{A D} = \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{A C}$，
所以$\left( 2 \overrightarrow{A D} \right)^{2} = \left( \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{A C} \right)^{2}$，
所以$4 \left| \overrightarrow{A D} \right|^{2} = \left( 2 \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right)^{2}$，
所以$\left| \overrightarrow{A D} \right| = \sqrt{\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|^{2} + \frac{\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2}}{4} - \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b}} = \sqrt{2^{2} + \frac{1^{2}}{4} - 2 \times 1 \times \frac{1}{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{13}}{2}$.
故答案为：$\frac{\sqrt{13}}{2}$
##### 【变式2】（2023下·江西上饶·高一校联考阶段练习）在菱形*ABCD*中，*O*为菱形*ABCD*内一点.
(1)用，$\overrightarrow{O B}$，$\overrightarrow{O C}$，表示$\overrightarrow{O D}$；
(2)若$A B = 2$，$\angle D A B = 6 0^{\circ}$，求$\left| \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{A D} \right|$，$\left| \overrightarrow{A B} - \overrightarrow{A D} \right|$.
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>(1)$\overrightarrow{O D} = \overrightarrow{O A} - \overrightarrow{O B} + \overrightarrow{O C}$
(2)$\left| \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{A D} \right| = 2 \sqrt{3}$；$\left| \overrightarrow{A B} - \overrightarrow{A D} \right| = 2$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>（1）因为四边形$A B C D$为菱形，所以$\overrightarrow{B C} = \overrightarrow{A D}$，
则$\overrightarrow{O C} - \overrightarrow{O B} = \overrightarrow{O D} - \overrightarrow{O A}$，所以$\overrightarrow{O D} = \overrightarrow{O A} - \overrightarrow{O B} + \overrightarrow{O C}$.
（2）因为$A B = 2$，$\angle D A B = 6 0^{\circ}$，
所以$\overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A D} = \left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow{A D} \right| \cos \angle D A B = 2 \times 2 \times \frac{1}{2} = 2$，
则$\left| \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{A D} \right| = \sqrt{( \overrightarrow{A B} + \overrightarrow{A D} )^{2}} = \sqrt{\left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right|^{2} + 2 \overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A D} + \left| \overrightarrow{A D} \right|^{2}} = \sqrt{4 + 4 + 4} = 2 \sqrt{3}$，
$\left| \overrightarrow{A B} - \overrightarrow{A D} \right| = \sqrt{( \overrightarrow{A B} - \overrightarrow{A D} )^{2}} = \sqrt{\left| \overrightarrow{A B} \right|^{2} - 2 \overrightarrow{A B} \cdot \overrightarrow{A D} + \left| \overrightarrow{A D} \right|^{2}} = \sqrt{4 - 4 + 4} = 2$.
