### 题型06 由向量的坐标求模
##### 【典例1】（2023下·黑龙江哈尔滨·高一哈师大附中校考阶段练习）已知向量$\overrightarrow{a} = ( 1 , 2 )$，$\overrightarrow{b} = ( 2 , 1 )$，则$\left| \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} \right| =$（ ）.
$\quad$A．$\sqrt{2}$ 
$\quad$B．$3 \sqrt{2}$
$\quad$C．$5 \sqrt{2}$ 
$\quad$D．$10 \sqrt{2}$
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>B
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>向量$\overrightarrow{a} = ( 1 , 2 )$，$\overrightarrow{b} = ( 2 , 1 )$，则$\overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} = ( 3 , 3 )$，
所以$| \overrightarrow{a} + \overrightarrow{b} | = \sqrt{3^{2} + 3^{2}} = 3 \sqrt{2}$.
故选：B

##### 【变式2】（2023上·陕西榆林·高三校考阶段练习）已知平面向量$\overrightarrow{a} = ( 2 , 0 ) , \overrightarrow{b} = \left( 1 , \sqrt{3} \right)$，则$\left| \overrightarrow{a} - 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right| =$ .
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>$2 \sqrt{3}$
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为$\overrightarrow{a} = ( 2 , 0 )$，$\overrightarrow{b} = \left( 1 , \sqrt{3} \right)$，
所以$\overrightarrow{a} - 2 \overrightarrow{b} = ( 2 , 0 ) - 2 \left( 1 , \sqrt{3} \right) = \left( 0 , - 2 \sqrt{3} \right)$，
所以$\left| \overrightarrow{a} - 2 \overrightarrow{b} \right| = 2 \sqrt{3}$.
故答案为：$2 \sqrt{3}$.
