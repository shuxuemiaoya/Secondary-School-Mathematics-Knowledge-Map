##### 题型04平面向量夹角（最值，范围）问题
##### 【典例1】（2023上·浙江·高三浙江省富阳中学校联考阶段练习）已知$\left| \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right| = 3$，$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| = 2 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|$，则$\text{cos} \left\langle \overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right\rangle$的最小值为（ ）
$\quad$A．$\frac{1}{2}$ 
$\quad$B．$\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ 
$\quad$C．$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ 
$\quad$D．1
<span class="fake-tag">答案</span>C
<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>由$\left| \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right| = 3$，$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| = 2 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|$可得$\left| \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} = \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|^{2} + \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} - 2 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 5 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} - 2 \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = 9$；
所以$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = \frac{5}{2} \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} - \frac{9}{2}$；
因此$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \left( \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right) = \overrightarrow{a}^{2} - \overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{b} = \left| \overrightarrow{a} \right|^{2} - \frac{5}{2} \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} + \frac{9}{2} = 4 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} - \frac{5}{2} \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} + \frac{9}{2} = \frac{3}{2} \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} + \frac{9}{2}$，
所以$\text{cos} \left\langle \overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right\rangle = \frac{\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \left( \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right)}{\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| \left| \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right|} = \frac{\frac{3}{2} \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|^{2} + \frac{9}{2}}{2 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| \times 3} = \frac{1}{4} \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| + \frac{3}{4 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|}$，
显然$\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| > 0$，所以$\frac{1}{4} \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| + \frac{3}{4 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|} \geq 2 \sqrt{\frac{1}{4} \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| \times \frac{3}{4 \left| \overrightarrow{b} \right|}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$，当且仅当$\left| \overrightarrow{b} \right| = \sqrt{3}$时，等号成立；
此时$\text{cos} \left\langle \overrightarrow{a} , \overrightarrow{a} - \overrightarrow{b} \right\rangle$的最小值为$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
故选：C
##### 【典例2】（2023下·重庆酉阳·高一重庆市酉阳第二中学校校考阶段练习）已知单位向量$\overrightarrow{e_{1}}$，$\overrightarrow{e_{2}}$的夹角为60°，向量$\overrightarrow{a} = x \overrightarrow{e_{1}} + y \overrightarrow{e_{2}}$，且$1 \leq x \leq 3$，$1 \leq y \leq 2$，设向量$\overrightarrow{a}$与$\overrightarrow{e_{1}}$的夹角为$\alpha$，则$\cos \alpha$的最大值为（ ）.

<span class="fake-tag">详解</span>因为单位向量$\overrightarrow{e_{1}}$，$\overrightarrow{e_{2}}$的夹角为$6 0^{\circ}$，
则$\overrightarrow{e_{1}} \cdot \overrightarrow{e_{2}} = \left| \overrightarrow{e_{1}} \right| \cdot \left| \overrightarrow{e_{2}} \right| \cos 6 0 {^\circ} = \frac{1}{2}$，
所以$\overrightarrow{a} \cdot \overrightarrow{e_{1}} = \left( x \overrightarrow{e_{1}} + y \overrightarrow{e_{2}} \right) \cdot \overrightarrow{e_{1}} = x \overrightarrow{e_{1}}^{2} + y \overrightarrow{e_{1}} \cdot \overrightarrow{e_{2}} = x + \frac{1}{2} y$，
又$\left| \overrightarrow{a} \right| = \sqrt{\left( x \overrightarrow{e_{1}} + y \overrightarrow{e_{2}} \right)^{2}} = \sqrt{x^{2} \overrightarrow{e_{1}}^{2} + 2 x y \overrightarrow{e_{1}} \cdot \overrightarrow{e_{2}} + y^{2} \overrightarrow{e_{2}}^{2}} = \sqrt{x^{2} + x y + y^{2}}$，

$\because \lambda \geq \frac{7}{4}$，$\therefore 3 < \frac{2}{\lambda} + 3 \leq \frac{8}{7} + 3 = \frac{29}{7}$，$\therefore \frac{4}{\frac{2}{\lambda} + 3} \geq \frac{28}{29}$，
即${} {\cos {}^{2}} \theta$的最小值为$\frac{28}{29}$.
故答案为：$\frac{28}{29}$.
