分析：只要具备什么条件，就能说明 $AD / / BC?$

$$
\angle B = \angle C,
$$

$$
\therefore \angle C = \frac {1}{2} \angle E A C _ {\circ}
$$

$AD$ 平分 $\angle EAC,$

$$
\therefore \angle D A C = \frac {1}{2} \angle E A C _ {\circ}
$$

$$
\therefore \angle D A C = \angle C _ {\circ}
$$

$$
\therefore A D / / B C _ {\circ}
$$
