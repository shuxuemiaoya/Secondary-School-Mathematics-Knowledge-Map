> [!example]- **例 3** 计算
> 1. $\left(-\frac{5}{6}+\frac{3}{8}\right)\times(-24)$；
> 2. $(-7)\times \left(-\frac{4}{3}\right)\times \frac{5}{14}$。
>
> > [!success]- 解
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{rl}
> > (1)\quad \left(-\frac{5}{6} + \frac{3}{8}\right) \times (-24)
> > &= \left(-\frac{5}{6}\right) \times (-24) + \frac{3}{8} \times (-24) \\
> > &= 20 + (-9) \\
> > &= 11.
> > \end{array}
> > $$
> >
> > $$
> > \begin{array}{rl}
> > (2)\quad (-7) \times \left(-\frac{4}{3}\right) \times \frac{5}{14}
> > &= (-7) \times \frac{5}{14} \times \left(-\frac{4}{3}\right) \\
> > &= \left(-\frac{5}{2}\right) \times \left(-\frac{4}{3}\right) \\
> > &= \frac{10}{3}.
> > \end{array}
> > $$


> [!question] 思考·交流
> 下面是计算 $\left(\frac{1}{3} +\frac{1}{4} -\frac{1}{6}\right)\times 24$的两种解法。
>> [!success]- 解法一
>> $$
>> \begin{aligned}
>> \left(\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{6}\right) \times 24 &= \left(\frac{4}{12} + \frac{3}{12} - \frac{2}{12}\right) \times 24 \\
>> &= \frac{5}{12} \times 24 \\
>> &= 10.
>> \end{aligned}
>> $$
> 
>> [!success]- 解法二
>> $$
>> \begin{aligned}
>> \left(\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{6}\right) \times 24 &= \frac{1}{3} \times 24 + \frac{1}{4} \times 24 - \frac{1}{6} \times 24 \\
>> &= 8 + 6 - 4 \\
>> &= 10.
>> \end{aligned}
>> $$
> 
> 比较两种解法，说说它们的区别，并与同伴进行交流。
