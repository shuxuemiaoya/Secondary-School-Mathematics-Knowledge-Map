> 
> 解：∵ $\angle A + \angle B + \angle C + \angle D = (4-2) \times 180^{\circ} = 360^{\circ}$ ，
> 
> $$
> \begin{array}{rl}
> \therefore & \angle B + \angle D \\
> & = 360^{\circ} - (\angle A + \angle C) \\
> & = 360^{\circ} - 180^{\circ} \\
> & = 180^{\circ} .
> \end{array}
