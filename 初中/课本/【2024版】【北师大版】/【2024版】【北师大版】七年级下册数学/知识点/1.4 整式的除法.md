> [!example]- 例题
> 计算：  
> 1. $-\frac{3}{5}x^{2}y^{3}\div3x^{2}y$  
> 2. $10a^{4}b^{3}c^{2}\div 5a^{3}bc$  
> 3. $(2x^{2}y)^{3} \cdot (-7xy^{2}) \div 14x^{4}y^{3}$  
> 4. $(2a + b)^{4} \div (2a + b)^{2}$  
> 5. $(9x^{2}y - 6xy^{2})\div 3xy$  
> 6. $\left(3 x ^ {2} y - x y ^ {2} + \frac {1}{2} x y\right) \div \left(- \frac {1}{2} x y\right)$
> 
> > [!success]- 解
> > 1. 
> > $$
> > \begin{aligned}
> > -\frac{3}{5}x^{2}y^{3}\div3x^{2}y &= \left(-\frac{3}{5}\div3\right)x^{2-2}y^{3-1} \\
> > &= -\frac{1}{5}y^{2}
> > \end{aligned}
> > $$
> > 
> > 2. 
> > $$
> > \begin{aligned}
> > 10a^{4}b^{3}c^{2}\div 5a^{3}bc &= (10\div 5)a^{4 - 3}b^{3 - 1}c^{2 - 1} \\
> > &= 2ab^{2}c
> > \end{aligned}
> > $$
> > 
> > 3. 
> > $$
> > \begin{aligned}
> > (2 x ^ {2} y) ^ {3} \cdot (- 7 x y ^ {2}) \div 14 x ^ {4} y ^ {3} &= 8 x ^ {6} y ^ {3} \cdot (- 7 x y ^ {2}) \div 14 x ^ {4} y ^ {3} \\ 
> > &= [ 8 \times (- 7) \div 14 ] x ^ {6 + 1 - 4} y ^ {3 + 2 - 3} \\ 
> > &= - 4 x ^ {3} y ^ {2}
> > \end{aligned}
> > $$
> > 
> > 4. 
> > $$
> > \begin{aligned}
> > (2a + b)^{4} \div (2a + b)^{2} &= (2a + b)^{4 - 2} \\
> > &= (2a + b)^{2} \\
> > &= 4a^{2} + 4ab + b^{2}
> > \end{aligned}
> > $$
> > 
> > 5. 
> > $$
> > \begin{aligned}
> > (9x^{2}y - 6xy^{2})\div 3xy &= 9 x ^ {2} y \div 3 x y - 6 x y ^ {2} \div 3 x y \\
> > &= 3 x - 2 y
> > \end{aligned}
> > $$
> > 
> > 6. 
> > $$
> > \begin{aligned}
> > \left(3x^{2}y - xy^{2} + \frac{1}{2}xy\right) \div \left(-\frac{1}{2}xy\right) &= 3x^2y \div \left(-\frac{1}{2}xy\right) - xy^2 \div \left(-\frac{1}{2}xy\right) + \frac{1}{2}xy \div \left(-\frac{1}{2}xy\right) \\
> > &= -6x + 2y - 1
> > \end{aligned}
> > $$
